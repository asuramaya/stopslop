## Technical Debt

Nobody plans to take on technical debt. It shows up anyway — in the Friday hotfix that never got a second pass, in the config flag someone added for one customer in 2021 and everybody has been too polite to remove since.

The metaphor gets abused. Debt implies you borrowed something on purpose and know the terms, and most of what we call debt is closer to sediment: it settled. Nobody signed for it. There's no interest rate you can look up, only the growing sense that a change which used to take a day now takes a week, and nobody can say exactly when that happened.

This issue is about getting specific. Three teams walk through a system they were afraid of, what it actually cost them to keep it, and what they did about it — including one case where the answer was to leave it alone and write down why.

We're not going to tell you to allocate 20% of every sprint to cleanup. You've read that advice. It doesn't survive contact with a launch date. What we do have is a handful of engineers describing, concretely, how they made the argument to ship less this quarter and won.
