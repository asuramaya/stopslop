Chicago or remote (US) · $195k–$235k + equity

Six-plus years writing backend services, and real time spent on systems where money or its equivalent had to balance. Payments experience is a nice-to-have; distributed systems judgment is not. You should be comfortable owning something on-call and comfortable saying no to a launch date.

We write Go, but we hire people who've written Java, Elixir, Rust. Language is learnable. Knowing why a retry storm happens at 2 a.m. is less so.
