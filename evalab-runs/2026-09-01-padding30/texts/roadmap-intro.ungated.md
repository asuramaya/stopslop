We've never published a roadmap before. The argument against it was the usual one: plans change, dates slip, and a public list of promises turns into a public list of things you didn't ship. All true. We're doing it anyway.

Here's the trade we're making. You're building on top of us, and that means you need to know whether the thing you're working around this quarter is something we're about to fix, or something we've quietly decided to live with. Guessing at that is expensive. Filing an issue and waiting eight months to find out is worse.

So this page lists what we're working on now, what we expect to start next, and a longer section of things we think matter but haven't scheduled. No dates on anything past the current quarter — we'd only be making them up. Items move backward sometimes. When they do, we'll say why in the changelog rather than deleting the row and hoping nobody noticed.

Two things this page isn't: a contract, and a complete list. Security fixes and small repairs ship continuously and mostly don't show up here.

Tell us what's missing. The feedback link on each item goes to the team that owns it.
