## Tack — team chat that keeps a record

Most chat tools are built for the conversation. Tack is built for the week after it.

**Threads that end somewhere.** Close any thread with a one-line outcome, and the outcome is what surfaces in search — not the forty messages that produced it. Postmortems stop being archaeology.

**Full history on every plan.** Including the free one. We don't meter your team's memory and sell it back at $12 a seat.

**SSO and SCIM at the base price.** No enterprise tier for the security review you already have to pass. SAML, SCIM provisioning, and an exportable audit log, from day one.

**Quiet unless it matters.** Channel notifications are off by default. You opt in per channel, or get pinged when someone names you. That's the whole model.

**Native to your stack.** GitHub, Linear, PagerDuty, and Sentry are first-party, not community webhooks someone abandoned in 2023. An incident in PagerDuty opens a channel, pulls the on-call, and writes a timestamped timeline you can export as Markdown.

**Migration takes a weekend.** Point us at a Slack export. Threads, timestamps, and file attachments come across intact. Channel names too, so nobody has to relearn where anything lives.

Tack is $7 per person per month, billed annually. No per-message limits, no add-on for guests, no separate SKU for compliance.
