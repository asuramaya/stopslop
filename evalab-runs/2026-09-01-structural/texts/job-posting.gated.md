Senior Backend Engineer
Remote (US) or Chicago office · $185–215k + equity

We move money. About 40,000 small businesses run payroll through our API, which means a bad deploy on Thursday afternoon shows up in someone's rent check on Friday. That pressure shapes how we work. Small PRs, real code review, no heroics at 2 a.m.

The stack is Go and Postgres, with Kafka between the ledger service and everything downstream. You'd own one or two services end to end — design, on-call, the postmortem when it breaks. Right now the biggest open problem is our reconciliation pipeline, which was written for 300 customers and is now doing daily batch work that wants to be incremental.

We want five-plus years writing backend services that other people depended on. You need comfort with SQL beyond ORM defaults, enough to read an EXPLAIN plan and have opinions about it. Experience with money, ledgers, or anything else where "eventually consistent" is a legal problem and not just an engineering one helps a lot, though the appetite to learn it fast counts too.

We don't care about your degree. We do care that you can explain a technical decision to a compliance analyst without condescending to them.

The interview is a 30-minute call with the hiring manager, a two-hour paired session on a real problem from our backlog, and a conversation with two engineers you'd work beside. Three steps, done in about two weeks. No take-home longer than the paid one, and yes, we pay for it.

Apply with a résumé and a note about something you built.
