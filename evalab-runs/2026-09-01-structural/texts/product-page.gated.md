# Relay

Team chat for people who get paged.

Every chat tool demos well. The real question is what your team's Tuesday looks like six months in: forty channels deep, three of them load-bearing, and a new hire who can't find the deploy runbook. Relay is built for that Tuesday.

## Threads that don't rot

Permanent URLs, full-text search back to day one, no 90-day cap, no paywall on your own history.

## On-call that respects sleep

Route alerts by rotation from PagerDuty or Opsgenie; everything else waits until morning. Notification rules live in a YAML file in your repo. They get code review like anything else, and reviewers argue about them like anything else. When someone's night gets wrecked, you can point at the line that did it, and the fix ships as a pull request instead of a support ticket.

## Incidents in one place

Type `/incident`. Relay opens a channel, pins the timeline, and drops a Markdown postmortem draft when you close it. We built this because we kept doing it by hand.

## Your compliance team can stop emailing you

SSO and SCIM on every plan, including the cheap one. Audit log export, US or EU data residency, per-channel retention you set yourself.

## Migration is a weekend, not a quarter

Import from Slack or Teams with history, files, and threads intact. Run both in parallel until you're sure.

## What it costs

$7 per person per month, billed annually. Bots and guests are free. No per-message API pricing, and no "contact sales" tier holding SSO hostage.

Thirty days free, no card.
