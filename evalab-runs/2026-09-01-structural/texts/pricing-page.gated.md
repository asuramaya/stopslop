# Pricing

Three tiers. No per-review credits, no usage meter ticking in the corner.

## Solo — $0

One person, unlimited public repos, one private. Every push gets reviewed. No card. The free reviewer is the same reviewer the paid tiers get; it is just pointed at less code. We don't throttle you into upgrading.

## Team — $19 per contributor per month

You pay for people who opened a pull request that month. Nobody else. Everything in Solo, plus private repos without a cap, house rules you write in plain English ("never call the billing API from a request handler"), and three years of searchable review history. Blocking checks are opt-in, per branch, so a flaky rule can't hold the release hostage. A team of eight usually lands near $110.

## Enterprise — from $12,000 a year

Runs in your VPC or on your own metal. SAML or OIDC, audit logs that export straight to your SIEM, a written SLA with numbers in it. You get a named engineer who has actually read your codebase before the first call. We have filled out a few hundred security questionnaires and know our way around procurement paperwork.

Every tier reviews the same way — full repo context, not just the diff — and every tier can be cancelled from the settings page without an email to anyone.

Annual billing takes two months off Team. Students and maintainers of public projects get Team free; send us a link to the repo.
