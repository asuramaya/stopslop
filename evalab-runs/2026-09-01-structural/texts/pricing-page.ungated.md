# Pricing

Pick a tier. Switch whenever you like — we prorate the difference the same day.

**Solo — free**
One person, one repo. Every push gets a review in about 90 seconds, from the same engine the paid tiers run. What you give up is memory: findings expire after 30 days and the reviewer starts each week with a blank slate.

**Team — $19 per developer, per month**
Unlimited private repos, plus the thing that makes reviews stop being noise. The reviewer reads your last six months of merged PRs and quits flagging the patterns your team already decided were fine. Write custom rules in YAML. Pipe findings to Slack or Linear. You can make a failed review block the merge, though we ship that switch off, because most teams hate it in week one and turn it on in month three.

**Enterprise — talk to us**
Runs in your VPC or on your own hardware. No code leaves your network, ever. SSO, SCIM, seven years of audit logs, and a shared Slack channel with a four-hour response during your working hours. Pricing opens around $40 per developer and moves with seat count and how much of the deployment you want us to operate.

Two more things. Annual billing knocks 20% off Team. And every tier, including the free one, gets the full rule engine — we don't hold back detection quality to sell you an upgrade.
