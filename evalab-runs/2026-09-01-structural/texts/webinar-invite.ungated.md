### The slow query in your ticket queue is probably not the problem

**Wednesday, October 15 · 1:00 PM ET · 60 minutes**

Most teams tune the query someone complained about. Meanwhile the thing actually eating the database is a 4 ms SELECT that runs 900,000 times an hour and never generates a complaint at all.

We're spending an hour on the unglamorous version of performance work. Reading an EXPLAIN plan without guessing. Deciding when an index earns its write cost and when it's just overhead you pay on every INSERT. Finding the N+1 your ORM hid from you. Knowing the difference between a query that's slow and a database that's saturated — because the fixes have nothing in common.

Bring a plan you don't understand. We'll take questions for the last twenty minutes and work through real ones on screen.

**Who this is for:** engineers who own a production database and don't have a DBA to hand it to. Postgres and MySQL examples throughout; the diagnostic habits carry over to either.

No slides about why performance matters. You already know.
