Introduction

This page shows what we're building, roughly in what order, and how confident we are about each item. We publish it because you plan around our tools: if the feature you need is six weeks out, you'll wait; if it isn't on the list, you'll route around it. Either answer beats silence.

Items under Now are in active development, with dates we expect to hit. Next is work we've committed to but haven't scheduled, and the order there will shift. Exploring is where we're still arguing internally. A fair amount of it will never ship, which is the intended outcome of that stage; killing an idea early is the point, not a failure. Some things are missing on purpose, too. We keep security work, most infrastructure migrations, and anything covered by a customer agreement off the public list. What's here is otherwise the same board our engineers work from.

Dates slip. When one does, we move the item and note why in the changelog at the bottom of the page rather than quietly redrawing the timeline. You can see the history of every date we've missed, which is the honest way to read the ones we haven't.

Every entry links to its issue tracker thread. Argue with the priority there, add your use case, tell us we've weighted something wrong.
