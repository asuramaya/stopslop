## About Northgate

Northgate began at a front desk. Our founder spent two years managing check-in at a four-provider family practice in Dayton, watching a scheduling system built for restaurants get bent into a shape it was never meant to hold. Double-booked physicals. A recall list kept on index cards. A patient who waited eleven weeks for an appointment that had been cancelled, without notice, by a software sync error.

We built Northgate to fix that, and we have kept the scope narrow on purpose.

Northgate does clinic scheduling and nothing else. Appointment types carry honest durations. Provider templates account for lunch and charting time. Waitlists fill the slot a cancellation opens, and reminders get read. It talks to your EHR; it does not try to become one. Most of our team has worked in a clinic or built for one, so our support staff can tell you why a 15-minute follow-up runs to twenty-two — they have sat in that chair. When you call, you reach someone in Ohio who knows what a superbill is.

We sell to independent practices and small multi-site groups, generally between three and forty providers. Enterprise health systems have procurement departments and eighteen-month rollouts. The clinics we serve need something working by the end of the month. Pricing is per provider, published on the site, with no implementation fee.

About 900 clinics use Northgate today. If yours is still running on a system that fights you every Monday morning, we would like to hear about it.
