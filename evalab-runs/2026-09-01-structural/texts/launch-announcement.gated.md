# Dark mode is here

We shipped dark mode today.

It took longer than it should have. The first version was just an inverted palette, and it looked awful. Pure white text on pure black burns at 11pm, which is when most of you actually write. So we started over. Body text sits at 88% opacity against a #16181c background, and headings get the full white. Your eyes stop straining after about a minute.

Some details we sweated:

- Code blocks and inline `code` got their own syntax theme, not a recolored version of the light one.
- Images with transparent backgrounds no longer vanish. We put a subtle plate behind them.
- Highlights keep their hue. Your yellow highlighter is still yellow, just dimmer.

Turn it on under Settings → Appearance. Three options: Light, Dark, and Match System. Match System follows your OS schedule, so notes go dark when the rest of your desktop does.

Editing at night was the top request in our feedback inbox for two years running, with 1,400 votes. Sorry it took this long.

We skipped per-note themes. Several of you asked for them. We tried it, and a notebook where every page looks different is hard to read. If you disagree, tell us why.

Dark mode is in version 4.2, rolling out on iOS, Android, and web this week. Desktop follows next Tuesday.
