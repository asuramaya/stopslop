## Dark mode is live in Margin 2.4

It took us fourteen months. Not because dark mode is hard — because our first three attempts looked like someone had inverted a screenshot.

The version that shipped this morning is a real second theme. Every surface got repainted by hand. Body text sits at `#C9C9C9` against `#16181C`, not white on black, which is what gave those early builds their glare. Your highlighter colors were remapped rather than dimmed, so the yellow you used last March still reads as yellow. Code blocks use a separate syntax palette tuned for the dark background. Images in your notes get a faint border now, because a white PNG floating in a dark note looks like a hole.

Margin follows your system setting by default. If you'd rather pin it, Settings → Appearance has Light, Dark, and a schedule that flips at sunset based on your device's location. Nothing leaves the device to make that work.

Two things we did not do. There's no AMOLED true-black mode yet — pure `#000000` made scrolling text smear on the phones we tested. And PDF previews still render light, since we're at the mercy of the system viewer there. Both are on the list.

This is the most-requested feature in our tracker, by roughly four to one. Thanks for the patience, and for the eleven bug reports from beta testers who caught the contrast problems in tables before anyone else saw them.
