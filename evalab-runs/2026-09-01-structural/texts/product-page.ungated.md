# Halyard

**Chat for teams that ship.**

You aren't shopping for another place to type. You already have three. What you want is fewer interruptions per engineer per day, and a way to find the decision someone made eight months ago without asking them to remember it.

**Threads are the default, not the escape hatch.** Every message opens one. Channels stay skimmable, so the person back from two weeks off reads forty topics in ten minutes instead of scrolling four thousand lines.

**Search knows your repo.** Connect GitHub or GitLab and Halyard indexes commits, PRs, and issues next to messages. Search *why did we drop Redis* and you get the thread, the PR that did it, and the two people who argued about it.

Three things you get as a manager:

- **Notification budgets.** Cap how many pings a channel can send one engineer per day. The rest batch into an evening digest.
- **Focus windows from your calendar.** No DM lands during blocked time unless the sender marks it urgent, and urgent is rate-limited too.
- **Your data, on demand.** Full JSON export, any plan, no sales call.

Self-host against your own S3 bucket if compliance asks for it. SOC 2 Type II. SSO on every tier, including the free one, because charging for SAML is a tax on doing the right thing.

$7 per user per month. The Slack importer moves your history, threads intact, in about twenty minutes.

Start free for thirty days. No card.
