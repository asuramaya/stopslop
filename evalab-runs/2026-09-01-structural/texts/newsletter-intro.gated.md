## Interest, compounding

Nobody sits down and decides to take on technical debt.

It accumulates one reasonable Friday at a time: the migration deferred until after the launch, the test you meant to write, the config flag that was supposed to live for a sprint and is now four years old and load-bearing. The metaphor gets misused, too. Ward Cunningham coined it for shipping something you understand imperfectly and paying to correct your model later, a deliberate trade made with open eyes. What most of us call debt is closer to neglect, and the difference matters, because you can plan around a loan. You cannot plan around rot you have never counted.
