**Webinar: Database Performance Tuning**

Your queries ran fine at 10,000 rows. At 4 million, the same query takes 40 seconds and your on-call phone rings at 3 a.m.

Join us Thursday, October 9, at 1:00 p.m. ET. Priya Raman spent six years tuning Postgres and MySQL at a payments company that clears 12 million transactions a day. She will walk through the work she does when a customer reports a slow page.

She covers:

- Reading an EXPLAIN ANALYZE output without guessing
- The index you added that the planner ignores, and the reason it does
- Connection pool settings that cost you throughput
- The point where you stop tuning the query and change the schema

You leave with a checklist you can run against your own database on Monday morning.
