# Halo

Chat for teams that ship code.

Your engineers spend six hours a day in this window. Pick wrong and you will hear about it in retro after retro until you migrate again.

**Threads that survive the week.** A thread in Halo keeps its own history, its own participants, and a permalink you can paste into a ticket. You stop scrolling back through 400 messages to find the decision someone made on Tuesday.

**Incidents without the side channel.** Open an incident room straight from an alert. Halo pages the on-call engineer, pins the timeline, and drafts the postmortem from what people typed while the site was down. Your SREs stop keeping a second log in a doc nobody updates.

**Search that reads a stack trace.** Paste the exception. Halo surfaces the four conversations where someone hit it before, including the one from 2023 in a channel you archived.

**Admin you can hand off.** SCIM provisioning, audit export in CSV. Your IT lead configures it once and owns it. You stop approving guest accounts at 11pm.

**Pricing.** $7 per user per month. No message cap, no paywalled history. Retention runs as long as your policy says it does.

**Migration.** Import from Slack or Teams with messages and channel membership intact. Teams under 200 people finish over a weekend. Our engineer runs the import and stays in a shared channel with you until you sign off on it.

Try it with the whole team for 30 days. We will not ask for a card.
