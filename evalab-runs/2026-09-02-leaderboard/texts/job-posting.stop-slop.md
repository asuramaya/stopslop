**Senior Backend Engineer**

We move about $4B a year for small businesses that get paid by invoice. Our payments platform runs on Go and Postgres, with a Python service handling ledger reconciliation. You would own a slice of that: the parts that decide whether a payment settles, retries, or gets held for review.

The work is concrete. Last quarter our team cut settlement latency from 40 seconds to under 6 by rewriting how we batch ACH files. This quarter we are splitting the ledger service out of the monolith without taking downtime. You will write the migration plan, run it, and carry the pager the night it ships.

We want someone with six or more years writing production backend code, ideally where money or medical records were on the line. You should be comfortable reading a Postgres query plan, arguing about idempotency keys, and telling a product manager that a deadline needs to move.

We are 140 people, 30 in engineering. Code review is required, and reviewers respond within a day. We deploy about eight times a week. On-call is one week in six, and last month the rotation woke someone up twice.

Salary is $185,000 to $215,000 depending on where you land in our leveling rubric, plus equity. We are hybrid in Austin, three days in the office, and we do not hire fully remote for this role.

The process: a 45-minute call with the hiring manager, a take-home you can finish in three hours (we pay $500 for it), and a half day onsite with four people you would work beside. We decide within a week of the onsite.
