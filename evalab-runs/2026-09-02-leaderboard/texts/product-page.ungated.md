# Kestrel — chat for teams that ship

You are not really comparing chat apps. You are choosing what happens to your team's attention over the next two years.

Most tools optimize for the person typing. Kestrel optimizes for the person who opens it at 9am with 400 unread messages and a standup in ten minutes.

**Threads stay threads.** A reply never leaks back into the channel, so nobody has to police it.

**Search that knows your repo.** Link a PR and the conversation is indexed with it. Ask "why did we revert that" and you get the decision, not the twelve messages of confusion around it.

**Notification rules you can write down.** Per channel, per hour, per keyword, with org defaults an admin sets once — so a new hire is not on call for #random on day one.

**An export you would accept.** Full history in JSON, on demand, no sales call. If we get worse, leaving costs you an afternoon.

### For whoever signs the invoice

SSO and SCIM on every paid tier, not only the enterprise one. Audit logs you can stream to your own bucket. Self-host if you have to; the image we publish is the image we run.

### What we are bad at

No voice channels yet. The mobile client is adequate. If your team lives in all-day huddles, wait two releases and check back.

Start a 30-day trial. No card, and importers for Slack and Discord exports.
