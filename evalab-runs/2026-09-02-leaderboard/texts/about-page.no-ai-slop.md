# About

We make scheduling software for outpatient clinics: booking, provider calendars, waitlists, and reminders.

Rota started in 2019 at a family practice in Sacramento, where our co-founder Priya ran the front desk for six years. She was rebooking a cancelled 8:15 by hand, calling down a paper waitlist, when the practice manager asked why the $40,000 system couldn't do that. It couldn't. Most clinic scheduling tools were built as a side feature of a billing platform, and it shows.

So we started at the front desk instead of the ledger. When a patient cancels, Rota offers the slot to the three people most likely to take it and books whoever answers first. Providers change their own templates without filing a ticket. A new receptionist can work a full shift after about twenty minutes of training, which matters when front-desk turnover runs near 30% a year.

Today 340 clinics use Rota, most with two to twenty providers. We sell to independent practices and small groups, and we've turned down two acquisition offers from EHR vendors. We'd rather stay the part of the stack that has to work at 8am on a Monday.

We're 22 people. Half work in support and implementation, several have staffed a front desk themselves, and every engineer sits in on customer calls during their first month. When something breaks at your clinic, you talk to one of us, not a ticket queue.
