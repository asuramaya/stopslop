We shipped fourteen things last quarter and never told anyone about eleven of them. So here's the page.

Everything below is what we're actually building, ordered roughly by when we think it'll land. Roughly. Dates on a roadmap are guesses wearing a suit, and ours have been wrong by a month in both directions. The CLI rewrite got pulled forward when three customers hit the same config bug in the same week. Meanwhile our Terraform provider has been "next quarter" since February, which is embarrassing, and I'd rather say that out loud than quietly move the card.

Two things this page won't do. It won't list every internal refactor, because you don't care about our test harness. And it won't promise anything in the Exploring column. Those are ideas we're arguing about internally, some of which will die.

Why publish at all? Mostly because we kept getting the same question in support tickets and answering it privately, one person at a time, which is a dumb use of everybody's afternoon.

Items link to their GitHub issue. Comment there if we've got the priority wrong. We read all of it, we respond to maybe half, and we have changed the order at least four times because someone did exactly that.
