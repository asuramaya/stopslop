# Pricing

Every plan reviews pull requests on GitHub, GitLab, and Bitbucket, and posts inline comments within two minutes of a push.

## Free — $0

For solo work and open source.

- 200 reviewed pull requests a month
- One private repo, unlimited public ones
- Standard rules: security, performance, dead code
- Support on our Discord

## Team — $29 per developer per month

For teams of 3 to 50 who merge every day.

- Unlimited pull requests and private repos
- Custom rules written in plain English, scoped per directory
- The bot reads what your reviewers dismiss and stops flagging it
- 12 months of review history and per-repo stats
- Email support, answered within one business day

## Enterprise — custom, from $15,000 a year

For companies with compliance sign-off or self-hosted source control.

- Runs on your own hardware, air-gapped if you need it
- SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, audit logs
- Your code stays inside your network and never trains a shared model
- A named engineer, 4-hour response, quarterly rule tuning
