## What we're building next

This page is the honest version.

For the last two years we shipped in the dark and told you about it afterward. That worked when there were [400] of you. It doesn't work now — teams are betting release processes on our CLI, and they need to know six months out whether we're going to support the thing they're about to depend on. So we're opening the board.

The board has three columns, one for what's shipping now, one for the next two quarters, and one for the things we've argued about internally without deciding. That third column is the useful one. It's where you'll find remote build caching, the Windows runner, and the plugin API we keep half-designing and shelving. Nothing there is a promise. It's a list of what has our attention.

We update this monthly, on the first Tuesday, and we move items backward as well as forward. When something slips, the card says why. When we kill a feature, it stays on the page with a note, because a dead feature you can read about is more useful than one that quietly disappears.

Comment on any card. We read them before planning.
