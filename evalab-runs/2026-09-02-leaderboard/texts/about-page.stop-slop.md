# About

Anna Reyes ran the front desk at a family practice in Rochester for six years. A paper daybook, and a software package that took eleven clicks to move one appointment. In 2016 she quit and hired two engineers to build the thing she had wanted at that desk.

We still design around the front desk. Before we ship a feature, one of us sits with a scheduler through a Monday morning and watches where she gets stuck. We built most of the product out of those visits: a waitlist that fills a cancellation in under a minute, and a recall list a receptionist can work through between phone calls.

Our customers run small practices. Two doctors, sometimes twenty. They have no IT department, so we answer our own support line and a person picks up in under three minutes during business hours. We handle the data migration, and setup takes about a week.

We charge per provider, per month. Schedulers, billers, and front desk staff use the software at no extra cost, and we don't ask you to sign for a year. If you leave us, we hand you an export your next vendor can load.

Forty-one people work at Waypost. Nine of them scheduled patients for a living before they came here, and they still sit in on the Monday visits. We serve 340 clinics in eleven states. We have taken no outside money since a seed round in 2017, which means Anna and the staff decide what we build.
