# Pricing

## Solo — $0

For one person and up to three private repos. You get the full check suite, the same one the paid tiers run. No trial clock, no feature gates, no "upgrade to see your results" wall. What you don't get is history: results live for seven days, then they're gone. Solo exists because I got tired of tools that show you a red badge and then charge you to find out why it's red.

## Team — $12 per developer, per month

Unlimited repos. Ninety days of retained results, so you can point at a graph in a retro and say "yeah, that spike was the auth rewrite." Custom rule packs, shared across the org. GitHub and GitLab checks that block a merge when you want them to, and quietly annotate when you don't.

Billing counts developers who actually pushed code that month. Bots don't count. Interns who pushed once in March don't get billed in April.

## Self-hosted — starts at $4,000/year

Your infrastructure, your data, nothing leaving the network. Comes with a Helm chart, an air-gapped install path, and my phone number.

Priced per seat band rather than a flat rate, so a 40-person shop isn't subsidizing a 900-person one. Write to hector@ and tell me your headcount and I'll quote you the same day. If you're a nonprofit or a university lab, say so, the number changes.

---

Every tier runs identical analysis. The money buys retention, control, and where the thing lives. Not accuracy.
