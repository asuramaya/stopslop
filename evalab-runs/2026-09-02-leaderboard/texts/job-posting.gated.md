**Senior Backend Engineer**
Meridian Pay — Chicago or remote (US timezones)

We move about $4B a year for small and mid-sized businesses: payouts, ledgering, reconciliation. Roughly 180 people, 40 in engineering. We are past the stage where things are held together by one person's shell scripts, and not yet at the stage where you need three approvals to rename a column.

What you'd work on

Our ledger service is the center of the company. It's Go, backed by Postgres, and it settles a few hundred thousand transactions a day. You'd own a meaningful slice of it — likely the payout pipeline, which currently has a retry story we are not proud of. You'd also do API design for partner integrations, plus the unglamorous work of making money movement idempotent when a bank's endpoint times out mid-request.

What we're looking for

Five-plus years writing backend services that other people depend on. Strong Postgres — you should have opinions about isolation levels and have been burned at least once. Experience with financial data, or any domain where a wrong number is a real problem rather than a bad chart.

You don't need fintech on your résumé. You do need to be comfortable reading a regulation and turning it into a schema.

Practical details

$185k–$225k plus equity. Four days a week of meetings-free time is a stated goal and mostly a real one. We're on call one week in six, and the pager is quiet.

Our interview process is a call with the hiring manager, a code review exercise on our actual codebase, and two technical conversations. Two weeks, start to offer.
