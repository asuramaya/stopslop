Your database is fine until the Tuesday it isn't. A query that ran in 40 milliseconds for two years starts taking nine seconds, and nobody changed it. The table just got bigger, the planner changed its mind, and now support is asking why checkout times out.

Join us Thursday, March 19, at 1pm ET for a 60-minute session on finding and fixing slow queries in production Postgres and MySQL.

We'll work through real EXPLAIN output from systems under load — not toy examples with four rows. Topics include reading a query plan without guessing, when an index helps and when it makes writes worse, why connection pool settings cause problems that look like slow queries, and how to tell a bad plan apart from a hardware ceiling.

Priya Raghunathan has spent eleven years on database teams at Shopify and Datadog, most of it on the side of the pager. She'll show the diagnostic order she actually uses: what to check first, what to ignore, and when to stop tuning and add hardware.

Bring a slow query. The last 15 minutes are open for questions, and we'll take live ones from the chat.
