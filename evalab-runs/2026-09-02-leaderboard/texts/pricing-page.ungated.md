# Pricing

Every plan reviews as many repositories as you have. You pay per person, not per repo.

## Solo — $0

One developer, public and private repos.

- Review on every pull request
- 200 reviews a month
- Findings ranked by severity, each with the failing case written out
- Python, TypeScript, Go, Rust, Java

No card, nothing to cancel.

## Team — $19 per developer, per month

Up to 50 seats.

- Unlimited reviews
- Your own rules: write a check once, apply it across every repo
- Blocking mode, so a bad review fails CI
- History, so you can see which rules fire and which never do
- Slack and GitHub Checks
- Email support, one business day

## Scale — talk to us

For teams with compliance or hosting requirements.

- Self-hosted, or in your own cloud account
- SSO, SCIM, audit log
- Retention you set, and a signed DPA
- Rule packs shared across every team
- A named engineer and a written support agreement

---

Annual billing takes two months off the Team price. Move up or down whenever you want; we prorate the difference and refund the rest. Open source projects get Team free, no application form. If a review is wrong often enough that your team stops reading them, tell us and we will refund the month.
