# Dark mode ships today

Open Settings, tap Appearance, and pick Dark. Your notes turn charcoal, and the app carries that choice across your phone, tablet, and laptop.

Our design team spent four months on the palette. The first build paired pure black with pure white text, and beta testers hated it. One of them, a grad student who writes lecture notes after midnight, said the screen felt like oncoming headlights. We moved the background to a soft near-black and pulled the body text down to 87% white. The glare dropped, and she read for an hour without squinting.

We also rewrote the color rules for everything you paste in. Code blocks use a warmer syntax theme that keeps strings and comments apart at low brightness. Highlighter colors got reworked from scratch, since the neon yellow from light mode turned into a smear on a dark background. Images and PDFs stay untouched, because dimming a photo of a whiteboard helps nobody.

If you set your phone to switch themes at sunset, our app follows. You can also lock it to Dark and ignore your system setting, which is what our engineer Priya does on her desktop at noon.

One thing we skipped: we did not build a scheduler with custom times. Two testers asked for it, and we would rather ship the palette now and see whether more of you want it.

Dark mode is live in version 4.2 on iOS, Android, macOS, and the web. Update, open a note, and tell us where the contrast still hurts.
