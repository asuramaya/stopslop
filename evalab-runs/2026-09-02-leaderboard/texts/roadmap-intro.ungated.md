This page is where our plan lives in public.

We ship a lot without saying much, and that has been a problem. People building on our CLI have learned about breaking changes from the changelog, which is far too late. So here is everything we have committed to for the next two quarters, plus the things we argue about internally and still have not settled.

Three columns. **Now** is under construction and has an owner. **Next** is scoped and starts this quarter. **Exploring** means we think the problem is real and we do not yet know the shape of the answer; treat those as questions, not promises.

Dates appear on items where we are confident enough to be held to them. Where there is no date, it is because we would be guessing, and a guess dressed up as a commitment is worse than silence.

One thing we will not do: quietly delete a card. If something dies, it moves to the archive with a note about why it died and what we learned.

Every card links to its issue. Argue there. Priority changes more often from a good comment than from anything said in a planning meeting.
