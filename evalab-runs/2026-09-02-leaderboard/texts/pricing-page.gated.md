# Pricing

Every plan reviews every pull request. What changes is how much of your history the reviewer gets to read, and who you can call when it's wrong.

Solo is free. One private repo, unlimited public ones. Reviews land as a comment within about 90 seconds of a push. You get the same checks the paid plans get: logic errors, unhandled cases, the function someone already wrote in a different file two weeks ago. No card.

Team runs $18 per contributing seat each month. Unlimited repos. The reviewer reads your last 12 months of merged PRs and learns what your team actually rejects, so it stops re-arguing the parameter order you settled in March. Owners get a ruleset file, per-directory severity, and a Friday digest of what slipped through. Seats are counted by who pushed code that month, so a dormant account costs you nothing. Email support, two business days.

Enterprise starts at $12,000 a year. Runs in your VPC or on your own hardware; the code never leaves. SAML, SCIM, audit logs piped to your SIEM. You get a named engineer, a four-hour response window, and a quarterly session where we tune the rules against your last 500 reviews. Volume pricing opens at 200 seats.

Annual billing takes 15% off Team. Nonprofits and classrooms pay nothing on either plan; just email us.

Swap in your product name and real numbers before this ships. The prices, the SLA, and the claim about reading 12 months of merged PRs are placeholders I invented to give the copy something concrete to stand on.
