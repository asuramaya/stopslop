# About

We build scheduling software for outpatient medical clinics. That is the whole product: a calendar that knows the difference between a 15-minute follow-up and a 90-minute procedure, and that does not fall apart when a provider calls in sick at 6 a.m.

Meridian started in 2019, after our founder spent two years as a practice manager at a four-physician orthopedic group in Rochester. The clinic ran on a scheduling module bundled with its EHR. Rebooking a cancelled slot took eleven clicks and a phone call. Nobody at the vendor had ever worked a front desk.

So we wrote our own, then rewrote it twice after watching schedulers use it. Today about 340 clinics run on Meridian, most of them between two and twenty providers. They book roughly 1.9 million appointments a year through it.

## What we care about

Speed at the front desk. A scheduler on the phone with a patient should be able to find, hold, and confirm a slot in under thirty seconds. We time this, and we publish the number in our release notes when it moves.

Fitting the clinic's rules rather than ours. Templates, block times, double-booking policies, and no-show rules vary by specialty and by physician. Meridian lets you encode yours instead of adopting a default.

Being honest about integration. We connect to Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, and NextGen. Some of those connections are cleaner than others, and our sales team will tell you which is which before you sign.

We are 31 people, based in Rochester and Chicago, and profitable since 2022. No outside investors, no plans for any.
