We publish this roadmap because our support inbox kept filling with the same question: is anyone working on this?

The list below covers the next three quarters. Items at the top have engineers assigned and a branch open. Items lower down have an owner and a design doc, and little else. We move things between those two states more than we would like, and we mark the change here within a week of the decision.

Dates are our best guess at the time of writing. We miss them. When we do, we write a line explaining what we hit, so you can judge whether to wait for us or work around us.

You can comment on any item, and two engineers read the comments each Monday. Four of the shipped features on this page began as a comment from someone running our CLI inside an air-gapped network, and one of them shipped six weeks later.

Some work stays off this page. Security fixes, infrastructure migrations, and anything a customer asked us to keep quiet under contract. We would rather leave a gap than post a vague placeholder you cannot plan around.

If you want a thing we have not listed, open an issue in the roadmap repo and tell us what breaks for you without it.
