Pricing

**Solo — $0**

One repo, unlimited reviews, one seat. The review runs on every push and comments inline on the diff. You get the full check set, the same one the paid tiers run. No credit card, no trial clock. Students and open source projects stay here forever.

**Team — $24 per developer per month**

Unlimited private repos. Add reviewers by GitHub org, and the tool picks up new members when your admin adds them. You write custom rules in YAML and commit them next to your code, so review policy lives in version control with everything else. Reviews block the merge when a rule is set to blocking, and your leads can override with a comment. Includes 90 days of review history and a weekly digest of which rules fired most.

**Enterprise — talk to us**

Everything in Team, plus SSO through Okta or your own SAML provider, audit logs you can export to Splunk or Datadog, and a self-hosted runner if your source can't leave your network. We assign a support engineer who answers in Slack within four hours during your business day. Contracts are annual. Pricing depends on seat count and whether you self-host.

**Notes**

Seats are named, not concurrent. Swapping a departing developer for a new hire costs nothing. Annual billing takes 15% off Team. If your monthly bill drops because your team shrank, we credit the difference on the next invoice instead of holding it.

Cancel from the billing page. Your review history stays available for 30 days after that, and you can export all of it as JSON.
