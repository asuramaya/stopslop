# Senior Backend Engineer

**Location:** Chicago or remote (US time zones)
**Salary:** $185,000–$225,000 + equity

We process about 4 million payments a month for small businesses that can't get a straight answer from their bank. Our core ledger runs on Go and Postgres, with Kafka moving events between roughly 30 services. You'd own a piece of that: payment orchestration, our reconciliation engine, or the risk-scoring pipeline, depending on where your interests land.

The work is unglamorous in the way finance infrastructure always is. Money moves, and it has to move exactly once, in the right amount, with a paper trail an auditor can follow three years later. Last quarter one of our engineers rewrote the retry logic on card authorizations and cut duplicate charges from 40 a week to two. That's the kind of win we celebrate.

**What we're looking for:**

- 6+ years writing backend services that handle real money or real consequences
- Deep Postgres knowledge: query plans, locking behavior, migrations that don't take the site down
- Experience with distributed systems failure modes (idempotency, exactly-once delivery, partial failures)
- Go, or strong systems experience in another language and the willingness to learn it

Nice to have: card network protocols, ACH, ledger design, or SOC 2 audit experience.

**How we work:** two-week cycles, no on-call rotation for your first three months, code review by humans who read the whole diff. We ship on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Nobody deploys on Friday.

**Interview process:** 30-minute call with the hiring manager, a two-hour technical session on a real problem from our backlog (paid), and a team conversation. Three weeks start to finish.

Apply at jobs@[company].com with anything you've built.
