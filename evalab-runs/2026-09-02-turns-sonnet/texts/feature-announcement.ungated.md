**Your notes now work without a connection.**

Local-first sync is live. Write, edit, and organize notes on a plane, in a basement, or anywhere your signal drops. Every change saves to your device the moment you make it, then reconciles with the cloud once you're back online.

If two devices edit the same note while both are offline, the app cannot pick a winner on its own. So it saves both versions and asks you to choose when you reconnect — you'll see each version's timestamp and device, and can pick one or merge pieces from both.

A few things worth knowing:

- Sync status shows in the note header: saved locally, syncing, or up to date.
- Attachments and images sync too, not just text.
- Conflict resolution is manual by design.

This has been our most requested feature. Update the app to get it — no setup required.
