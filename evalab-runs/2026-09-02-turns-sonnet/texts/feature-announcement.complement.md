Your notes now sync with local-first sync.

Write on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere the connection drops, and your changes save locally the moment you make them. When the network comes back, everything merges in the background. No spinner, no "reconnecting" banner, no lost paragraph.

If you edit the same note on two devices while both are offline, the app keeps both versions instead of overwriting one with the other. When the devices reconnect, you get a side-by-side comparison and pick which parts to keep, or keep both as separate notes.

This also fixes the flaky-wifi problem: spotty coffee-shop connections used to cause failed saves and duplicate notes. Now the app queues changes and sends them when the connection is stable, so a bad signal costs you nothing.

Local-first sync works across iOS, Android, and desktop, and rolls out to all users this week. No settings to change, no toggle to find. Open it anywhere, write, and trust it's there next time you open the app.
