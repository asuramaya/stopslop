On-call

You'll join the on-call rotation after your first shadow week, once your buddy signs off. Rotations run one week at a time, and the schedule lives in the payments-oncall calendar linked in #payments-oncall on Discord. Primary carries the pager; secondary backs them up and takes over if primary can't respond within 15 minutes. Every incident gets a postmortem in docs/incidents, written up within 48 hours regardless of severity.
