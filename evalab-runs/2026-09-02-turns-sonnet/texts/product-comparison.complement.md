For a small team: default to batch. It runs on cron infrastructure the team already has, needs no separate worker fleet, and a stuck job is a log to read the next morning. Add a queue only when a specific job runs within seconds of being created — a signup email, a webhook. One small worker pool with a dead-letter queue and an alert on its depth covers that case. Running both a queue and a complex batch pipeline before either is required adds two operational surfaces for a team that can staff one.
