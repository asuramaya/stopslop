Local-first sync now runs offline. Write on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere the connection drops, and every change lands the moment you're back online.

Edits save to disk on your device first, then queue for sync. Open the same notebook on your phone during a commute and on your laptop that evening, and both copies agree without you doing anything. If two devices edit the same note while both are offline, the app merges changes at the paragraph level and flags true conflicts for you to resolve, instead of picking a winner and discarding the other version.

We built this after hearing from users who lost edits during flaky hotel wifi or subway rides. A sync icon in the top bar shows status: a checkmark when everything's synced, a spinning arrow while changes are queued, a warning icon if a conflict needs your attention.

No setup is required. Update the app and local-first sync turns on for every note you own. Shared notebooks support it too, so a team can keep editing through a dropped connection and reconcile once everyone's back.
