For a small team, start with scheduled batch runs. A cron job needs no new infrastructure, fails in an obvious way, and covers most early needs — reports, cleanups, syncs. Add a queue only when a specific feature needs low latency, like a signup email or a payment webhook, and add it for just that path, not as a wholesale migration. Queue infrastructure — retries, dead-letter handling, worker deployment — is worth building once you know exactly which jobs need it, not before.
