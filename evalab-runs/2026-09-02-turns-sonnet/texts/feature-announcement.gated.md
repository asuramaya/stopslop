Local-First Sync Is Here

Your notes work the same whether you're on a plane, in a basement, or anywhere else the signal drops. Write, edit, and organize notes with no connection, and everything syncs the moment you're back online. Changes save locally right away, then merge with the cloud automatically, with no manual refresh and no lost work.

If two devices edit the same note while both are offline, the app does not silently overwrite either version. It keeps both copies and shows them side by side so you can merge them yourself or pick the one you want to keep.

Offline changes sync within seconds of reconnecting. Attachments download for offline access only after you open them once while connected. A small icon in the top bar shows each note's sync status: synced, pending, or in conflict.

This has been our most requested feature for two years. Update the app, and your notes will keep up with you.
