For a small team, use a queue for anything a user waits on, and cron for everything else. Managed queue services like SQS or Cloud Tasks need little setup, and the payoff — no dropped signups, no stuck emails — justifies that setup. Reserve batch jobs for work that's periodic and low-stakes if delayed: reports, cleanup, syncs. Don't build a queue for something that fits a nightly cron, and don't force user-facing work through a scheduled job to avoid running a worker.
