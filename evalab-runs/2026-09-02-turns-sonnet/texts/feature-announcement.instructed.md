Local-first sync means notes update on your device first and sync when a connection is available.

Write on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere without a signal, and the app queues your changes locally. When you reconnect, it merges them into your account, along with any edits made from another device while you were offline.

If two devices edit the same note while both are offline, the app keeps both versions and flags the note as conflicted until you resolve it. You'll see both edits side by side and choose which one stays. Neither is dropped or overwritten.

The sync queue is visible in settings, so you can check what's pending before you close the laptop. Data stays on the device until connectivity returns, and nothing is lost if the app closes or restarts mid-queue.

This ships today in version 4.2, for iOS, Android, and desktop. Update to get it.
