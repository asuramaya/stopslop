# Halyard

Your team runs on interruptions. Halyard prices them honestly.

**Channels that close.** Each channel gets an owner and a written scope. When the owner marks the scope done, Halyard archives the room and keeps it searchable. Your workspace stops accumulating four hundred dead channels over two years.

**Decisions, not scrollback.** Anyone in a thread can mark a message as the outcome. Halyard pins it with the date and the name of the person who called it. A new engineer reads eleven decisions instead of six thousand messages.

**On-call that respects sleep.** Halyard reads your PagerDuty schedule. Messages tagged urgent reach whoever holds the pager. Everything else waits until that person's working hours start, and the sender sees the delay before they hit enter.

**Search you can defend.** Filter by author, channel, date range, and file type at once. Results carry the thread they came from, so you read the argument and not the fragment.

**What we don't do.** No stories. No reaction leaderboards. No AI summary of a conversation you can read in forty seconds.
