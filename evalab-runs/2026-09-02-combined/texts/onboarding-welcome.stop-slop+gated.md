Welcome to Harborline

Your laptop is on the desk. The setup guide lives in the platform repo at docs/onboarding.md. Marisol on Platform wrote it and keeps it current. If a step breaks, fix the guide and open a pull request. That becomes your first merged change, and we count it as one.

Most people ship to production in week one. The pipeline takes about eleven minutes, and you roll back from the same screen you deployed from. You do not need approval to roll back. Break something, revert it, tell the room in #eng-incidents, and write up what you learned when you have the energy for it.

Your mentor for the first month is on your calendar already. They blocked two hours a day for you. Use the time. Ask about the parts of the codebase that make no sense, because three of us wrote the billing service in 2021 under a deadline and we know what it looks like from outside.

Standup runs at 9:45 for eight minutes. Skip it when you are heads down and post a line in the channel instead.

Two things we care about. Read the code around your change before you write it, and say when an estimate slips as soon as you know.

Lunch is on the company Tuesdays and Thursdays. Someone will drag you to the taco place on Third by Friday.

Priya, our CTO, will find you this afternoon. She asks new engineers the same question: what surprised you in your first eight hours? Write down the answer while you still notice things.
