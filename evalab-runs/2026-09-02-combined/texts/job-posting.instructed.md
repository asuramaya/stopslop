# Senior backend engineer

Toronto or remote within ±3 hours of Eastern time. $165k–$195k CAD, plus equity.

We move money for about 40,000 small businesses in Canada and the US: payroll runs, invoice settlement, and a card program we launched last year. Roughly 2.8 million transactions cleared last month.

## What you would work on

The ledger service is the center of everything. It is Go, Postgres, and a Kafka pipeline feeding reconciliation. It was written in 2021 by three people who have since left, and it is holding up better than it has any right to, but the double-entry model was designed before we had multi-currency and it shows. Your first six months would largely be untangling that without stopping the money.

After that, it depends on what you want. There is a fraud-scoring path, a bank-integration path (we talk to five clearing systems and each one is its own dialect), and an internal platform path.

## What we need

Seven or more years writing backend services, at least two of them somewhere correctness mattered more than velocity. Strong SQL. Comfort with Go, or enough systems experience that picking it up is a formality.

You have debugged a distributed system at 3am and can describe what actually went wrong.

Regulated-environment experience helps but is not required. We will teach you the compliance parts.

## Process

A 45-minute call with the engineering lead, a take-home you get paid for, a systems design conversation, and a half-day with the team. Four steps, about two weeks, and we tell you where you stand after each one.
