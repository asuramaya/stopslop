# What we're building

For seven years the honest answer to "what's next?" was a shrug and a link to the changelog. People kept asking anyway — in support threads, in issues we closed with *good idea, maybe someday*, in the occasional email from someone deciding whether to bet a migration on us. That's a fair question and we were bad at answering it.

So here's the board.

Three columns: **In progress** is work with people assigned and a branch open. **Next** is what we've committed to starting once something in column one ships. **Considering** is the interesting part — ideas we're arguing about internally, some of which we'll drop.

A few ground rules. We don't put dates on anything past the current quarter, because the last four times we did, we were wrong by an average of eleven weeks. Items move backward. Items get killed, and when one does we'll say why rather than letting it quietly vanish from the page. Nothing here is a contract, and none of it should show up in a procurement document.

Every card links to its issue. Comment there, thumbs it up, tell us we've ranked it wrong. We read all of it, and roughly a third of column three got there that way.
