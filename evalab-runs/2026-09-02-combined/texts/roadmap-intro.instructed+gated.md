# Roadmap

This page is what we're building next, in the order we expect to build it. It's public because our users are the people who file the issues, and it's easier to argue with a plan you can read.

Three things shape what's here. Support volume comes first, so if a problem generates enough tickets, it earns a place on this list ahead of things we'd rather work on. Second is the migration off the v1 API, which we've committed to finishing before the end-of-life date in March. Third is the pile of feature requests, which we read but do not treat as a vote count.

Dates are estimates, and we've been wrong about them before. Last year we shipped the workspace rewrite four months late because the storage layer needed replacing first. Items in the "next" column are funded and staffed. Items in "later" are directional, and roughly a third of them will get cut or replaced by something we haven't thought of yet.

We don't list security fixes or infrastructure work here. That happens continuously and doesn't queue behind features.

If something you depend on is missing, open an issue and say what breaks without it. That's the input that moves items around.
