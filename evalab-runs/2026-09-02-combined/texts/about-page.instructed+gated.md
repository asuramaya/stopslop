# About

We build scheduling software for medical clinics. That's the whole product. We don't do billing, we don't do charting, and we don't have a patient portal.

The company started in 2017 after our founder spent two years as a practice manager at a four-physician family medicine group in Cleveland. The scheduling system there was a module bolted onto an EHR that had been designed for hospitals. Booking a follow-up took eleven clicks. Rescheduling a whole afternoon after a physician called in sick took most of a morning on the phone.

Today about 900 clinics use our software, most of them independent practices with two to fifteen providers. Specialty mix skews toward primary care, dermatology, and physical therapy, though we have a handful of oral surgery groups too.

Some things we've learned and now design around: no-show rates drop when reminder timing matches how far out the appointment was booked. Front desk staff turn over often, so the software has to be learnable in an afternoon. Every clinic has scheduling rules that sound bizarre until you hear the reason, so we let you write your own instead of picking from a list.

We're 31 people, based in Cleveland with roughly half the team remote. We took a single round of funding in 2019 and have been profitable since 2022, which means we answer to clinics rather than to a growth timeline.

Support is staffed by people who have worked at a front desk. They answer the phone. If you want to talk to an engineer, we can usually arrange that too.
