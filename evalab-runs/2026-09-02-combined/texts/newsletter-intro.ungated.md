**This month: technical debt**

Nobody schedules it. That's the whole problem.

Debt doesn't appear in a roadmap review. It shows up on a Tuesday afternoon, when a two-line change takes four days because the config file you touched is read by seven services and owned by none of them. By then you've already paid. You're just finding out the amount.

We wanted a month on this without the usual framing, where debt is a discipline failure and the cure is trying harder. It isn't. Most of what we call debt is a decision that was correct when someone made it and quietly stopped being correct — a schema that fit the 2021 product, a queue sized for one region, a workaround for a vendor bug the vendor fixed in 2023.

Inside: one team's account of retiring a service nobody would admit to depending on, and what the dependency graph looked like once they went and checked. An argument for keeping some of your debt on purpose. And a short piece on writing the ticket, since the ones that get funded read like outage prevention, not like housekeeping.

Read it before your next planning meeting.
