# Dark mode ships today

Version 3.2 is live. Open Settings, tap Appearance, choose Dark. The app switches without a restart. If you'd rather let your phone decide, pick System and we follow your OS schedule at sunset.

Priya, our designer, spent six weeks on the palette. Her first version put white text on pure black, and beta testers told us the letters seemed to vibrate during scrolling. She lifted the background to #16161A and pulled the text down to a warm gray. Twelve testers then read a 2,000-word note on the new palette and reported less eye strain than the black version gave them.

Code blocks and highlights keep their hues, but we lowered the saturation so a yellow highlight no longer glows off the screen. Images stay untouched. We tried dimming them for two builds, then dropped the idea after Priya reminded us that photographers caption shoots in this app and need to trust what they see.

Export to PDF still gives you the light theme. Ask for dark PDFs and we'll build them.

Dark mode runs on iOS, Android and the web app now. Our desktop build lands in October, and Marcus has the branch open already.

We know about one bug. Stylus notes render on a white canvas instead of the dark one. Marcus ships the fix in 3.2.1 next week.

Update from the App Store or Google Play. If you don't see the Appearance toggle, force-quit the app and reopen it once. Reply to this post with your screenshots. We read them.
