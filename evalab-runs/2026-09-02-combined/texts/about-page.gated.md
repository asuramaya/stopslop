# About Meridian

We make scheduling software for medical clinics. Just that. No billing engine, no patient portal, no "platform." A scheduler, built properly.

Meridian started in 2019 in a spare room above a dental office in Akron. Our founder, Dana Reyes, had spent eleven years as a practice manager, and the last four of them fighting a system that couldn't handle a double-booked ultrasound room without a phone call to the vendor. She figured someone should fix that. Nobody did, so she did.

Today about 1,900 clinics run their day on Meridian. Family practices, dermatology groups, a few community health centers, one veterinary hospital that talked us into it. The software handles recurring appointments, provider blocks, room and equipment conflicts, waitlist backfill when a patient cancels at 7 a.m., and the particular mess of a physician who works Tuesdays at two locations.

We are twenty-six people. Nine of us worked in clinics before this.

Call support and you reach someone in Ohio who knows what a no-show rate is and why yours went up in January.

A few things we believe. Your schedule data is yours, exportable in one click, no sales call required. Pricing is posted on the site. We have never sold, shared, or brokered patient information, and we never will.

We are not trying to become the operating system for healthcare. We are trying to make sure the 2:15 goes to the right person, in the right room, with the right chart open.

That is enough of a problem.
