# Pricing

Every plan runs the same review engine. The difference is how many repositories it watches and who it answers to.

## Solo — $12/month

One private repository, unlimited public ones. Reviews open on every pull request and post as inline comments. You get the full check set: correctness, dead code, dependency drift, and the style rules you define in a config file. History goes back 90 days. Support is email, and I answer it myself, usually within a day.

## Team — $29 per developer/month

Up to 50 repositories, plus the things that only matter once more than one person is reviewing. Shared rule sets live in a central config repo, so a rule you write once applies everywhere. Reviews can block a merge when a check fails at error level. You can see which rules fire most across your codebase, which is the fastest way to find out that a rule is wrong rather than your code. History is unlimited. Two seats minimum.

## Self-hosted — from $8,000/year

The same engine as a container you run on your own hardware. Nothing leaves your network: no source code, no diffs, no telemetry. You get the license, the update channel, and a support contract with a four-hour response window during your business hours. Pricing scales with developer count and starts at 50 developers.

## Notes

Annual billing saves two months. Public open-source projects use Team features at no charge; email us with a link to the repo. You can cancel from the billing page, and unused time is refunded.
