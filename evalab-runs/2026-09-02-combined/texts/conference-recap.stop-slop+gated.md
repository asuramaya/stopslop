Three of us went to SaaStr last week. Here's what stuck.

The pricing talk from the Linear team changed how I think about our tiers. They charge per seat but cap the low tier at five seats, which pushes teams to upgrade before they feel squeezed. We cap nothing. Our mid-tier customers sit at 40 seats and pay the same as customers with 12. Priya ran the numbers on the flight home and found we're leaving about $180K a year on the table.

The other surprise was that nobody in the hallway conversations cared about AI features. I expected the opposite. Buyers asked about SOC 2 timelines, data residency, and whether we'd still exist in three years. Two prospects asked if we had a European region before they asked what the product did.

The support-automation session was thin. The speaker showed a deflection rate of 60% without saying what counted as deflection. Marcus asked during Q&A and got a non-answer.

We came back with one commitment, which is to rework the seat caps before Q4 pricing goes out. Priya owns it. Draft by the 19th.

Also worth saying, the conference itself was too big to run into people by accident. We scheduled every conversation we had. If we go next year, we should book the meetings first and treat the sessions as filler.
