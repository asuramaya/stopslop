Last month Priya spent nine days shipping a two-line change. Writing the lines took twenty minutes. The other eight days went to a config loader that three teams forked in 2022 and none of them maintains.

We heard versions of that story from four groups this quarter, so we made it the theme.

Engineers talk about technical debt like weather, something you endure and plan around. That framing hides the people. Someone took the shortcut during a sprint that was already late, and someone else approved the merge because the release train was leaving. You can put names to both decisions, and one of them is often yours.

Inside this issue: Marcus on the six weeks he spent deleting the feature-flag system he built in 2023, including the afternoon he discovered two customers still calling it. Wen and the payments team got budget for their rewrite by tracking on-call pages for a quarter instead of writing a proposal. Our infrastructure group published the spreadsheet they use to sort what they fix from what they keep, and they would like you to argue with the weights.

Read the pieces, then open the service you inherited and have not touched. Write back about what you found in there.
