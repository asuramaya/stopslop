Platform Engineering Charter

We build and run the road from a developer's laptop to production: CI pipelines, deployment tooling, service templates, observability, and the clusters underneath.

Product teams are our customers. We win when a team ships its second service without opening a ticket for us, and we lose when they wait on us for a database or a staging environment. We track the time from an empty repository to a running service, and we publish that number each month. That number is the one metric we will let you hold us to, because it moves only when the whole path gets shorter, and a team that has to wait three days for a cluster namespace will see it in the chart before we admit it in a standup.

We staff a rotation, not a queue. One person handles interrupts each week so the rest of us can finish work that takes more than a day. Bring us the paved road you want, and we will tell you the week we can start on it.

We do not own product code.

We will pair with you on the platform piece, review your service definition, and fix the tooling that got in your way. Self-service is our default. When a team asks us for the same thing twice, the third request goes through an API or a template, and we document what we build before we announce it.

We keep an escape hatch. A team that needs to leave the paved road can, and we ask that they tell us what pushed them off it, so we can widen the road later.

Reid owns the roadmap. Priya runs the on-call rotation and the reliability budget. Both answer to the teams we serve, and we will publish our quarterly commitments where you can read them.
