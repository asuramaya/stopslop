Dark mode ships today in version 3.4.

Open Settings, tap Appearance, pick Dark. Your notes keep their formatting, and the app remembers the choice across devices.

We took longer than you wanted. Our first attempt inverted the existing colors, and we pushed it to beta in March. Testers wrote back within a day: code blocks turned to gray mush, and images with white backgrounds sat on the page like flashlights. We threw that build away.

The version you get today uses a palette our designer, Ana, built from scratch. She set the background at a soft charcoal instead of pure black, because pure black under white text makes letters smear on OLED screens. Highlighter colors got new tints. Images now sit behind a dimming layer that you can switch off per note when you want the original back.

Two options live under Appearance. "Dark" holds the app dark no matter what your phone is doing. "Match system" follows your OS schedule, so the app goes dark at sunset along with everything else.

Some rough edges remain. PDF previews still render on a white page, and the print-export view ignores your theme. We plan to fix both in 3.5, which goes out in October.

Update from the App Store or Google Play. On desktop, the app pulls the new build when you restart it.
