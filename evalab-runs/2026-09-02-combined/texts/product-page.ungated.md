# Halyard

**Chat for teams that ship.**

Most chat tools are built for the whole company, then sold to you. Halyard was built for the eight-person team that keeps a service up at 3 a.m.

**Threads that survive the incident.** Every thread gets a permanent URL and a plain-text export. When you write the postmortem two weeks later, the conversation is still there, in order, with timestamps you can paste straight into a timeline.

**Search that knows what a stack trace is.** Paste 200 lines of Java into a channel and Halyard indexes the exception class, not the whole blob. Searching `NullPointerException OrderService` finds the Tuesday you already solved this.

**Quiet by default.** There is no @channel. You can set a team-wide focus window; messages queue and land at the end of it. On-call pages route through PagerDuty and ignore the window, because they should.

**Costs you can predict.** $6 per person per month, flat. Guests are free. There is no tier that unlocks message history. You keep all of it, forever, on every plan.

What we don't do: video calls, whiteboards, a document editor, an app store. We connect to the tools you already pay for — GitHub, Linear, PagerDuty, Datadog — and we stay out of the rest.

Halyard runs in us-east, eu-central, or on your own Kubernetes cluster. SOC 2 Type II. SAML and SCIM on every plan, not just enterprise.

Try it with your team for 30 days. No card.
