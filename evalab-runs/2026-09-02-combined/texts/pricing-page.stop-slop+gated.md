# Pricing

## Solo — $12/month

One reviewer seat, unlimited private repos. Every pull request gets a full pass: logic errors, unsafe type coercions, dead branches, tests that assert nothing. You see the comments inline in GitHub or GitLab before your teammates do. Cancel from the billing page in two clicks and we stop charging that day.

## Team — $29 per seat/month

Everything in Solo, plus rules your team writes. You define what counts as a violation in a YAML file, we enforce it on every branch, and you get a weekly digest showing which rules fire most and which ones nobody has hit in six months. Prune those. Admins control who can override a blocking review, and we log each override with a reason.

Teams under five people pay for three seats minimum. Above 40 seats, talk to us and we will quote you.

## Enterprise — quoted

You run the reviewer inside your own VPC or on your hardware. No code leaves your network. We ship signed container images, you pin the version, and you upgrade when your security team clears it. SSO through Okta, Entra, or any SAML provider. Audit logs stream to your SIEM in the format you already parse.

Your engineers get a named support contact who answers in Slack within four business hours. We will also sit with your platform team for two days to tune the rule set against your existing codebase, at no extra charge.

## Every tier

All tiers include a 14-day trial. We do not ask for a card up front, and we do not train models on your source code.
