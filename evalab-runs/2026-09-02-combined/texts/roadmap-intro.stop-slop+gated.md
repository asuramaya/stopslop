We ship a lot without telling anyone, so you find out when your build breaks or when a customer asks why the CLI grew a new flag. We're fixing that here.

Below is what our team is building over the next two quarters, ordered by when we expect to ship. Dates slip. When a security fix eats a sprint, we push items down and note the new date along with a sentence about why. Read "In progress" if you want to know what lands this quarter. Read "Exploring" if you want to argue with us before we write the code. Those items have no owner and no date, and your comment on one carries more weight now than it will in six months.
