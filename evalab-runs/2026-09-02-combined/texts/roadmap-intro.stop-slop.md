We keep this page open so you can plan against our work instead of guessing at it.

The list runs in three states. "Building" covers work our engineers have started, with the release we expect it in. "Next" is what they pick up when the current work lands. Items under "Considering" have no commitment behind them, and we will drop some of them.

Dates slip. When one moves, we edit this page and post the reason in the changelog. We would rather show you a date that shifted than one that went stale six weeks ago.

Most of what sits here came from you. We pull from the GitHub tracker and the support queue. About a third of these items trace back to teams who wrote to us after hitting a limit in production, usually around build times or the size of a single artifact. To get something on the list, open an issue and tell us what broke.

A few things stay off the page. We do not publish security work before it ships, and we hold back items that our partners asked us to keep quiet until their own launches.

Treat this as a plan. Hold us to the parts we marked committed.
