Ledgerline moves to a new platform on 14 January 2027

We run Ledgerline on hardware we bought in 2019. A report that took four seconds in 2021 now takes forty. On 14 January we move your account to the platform we built to replace it.

Our engineers copy your data between 11pm and 4am UTC on 13 January. Your saved reports and your team's permissions come across with it. You keep the same login and the same URL. During those five hours you cannot sign in.

Two things change. The CSV export drops the legacy "account_ref_old" column, which we duplicated from "account_ref" back in 2022. The v1 API stops accepting requests on 28 February, six weeks after the move, so you have time to point your integrations at v2. Priya on our support team wrote the migration notes at ledgerline.com/v2-api.

Your price does not change and neither do your contract terms. If you pay by invoice, the January invoice arrives on the usual date from the same address.

We tested the move with 40 accounts in November and hit two problems, both in the way we handled deleted users. We fixed those in December and ran the test again across 300 accounts. That second run turned up nothing.

Write to support@ledgerline.com if you want us to move your account on a different night. We can schedule it for any evening between 7 and 20 January, and we will confirm the slot within one working day.
