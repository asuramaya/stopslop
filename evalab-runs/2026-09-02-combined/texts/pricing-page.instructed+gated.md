# Pricing

Three tiers. Every one of them runs the same review engine — the difference is how many repos it watches and who it answers to.

## Solo — $0

For one person and up to three private repos. You get pull request comments on every diff, the full rule set, and the CLI so you can run the same checks locally before you push. No credit card, no trial clock. If you never outgrow it, stay here.

## Team — $18 per developer per month

Unlimited private repos, plus the parts that only matter once more than one person is reading the output. Shared rule configuration lives in a file in your repo, so a rule change goes through review like any other change. Reviews block merges when you tell them to and stay advisory when you don't. The history view shows which rules fire most, which ones people dismiss, and which files keep coming back.

Teams above 40 developers get volume pricing; write to sales@example.com with your headcount.

## Enterprise — talk to us

Self-hosted or in your own cloud account, SAML and SCIM, audit logs that export to your SIEM, and a written data processing agreement. You can write custom rules in Python against our AST API and ship them to every repo in the org from one place. Support is a shared Slack channel with a four-hour response target during your business hours.

Annual billing on Team and Enterprise saves two months. Switching down a tier takes effect at the end of the billing period, and your data comes with you either way.
