# Keel

**Chat that keeps the decision, not just the conversation.**

Your team already has a chat tool. The real question is what it costs you: the Monday morning someone spends scrolling to find why you picked Postgres over DynamoDB, the new hire who reads 4,000 messages and still asks in standup.

Keel treats a thread as the unit of work, not the channel. Every thread has an owner, a status, and a resolution field. When it closes, the summary is what search returns first — so "why did we do this" gets one answer instead of forty messages of context.

**Search that indexes your code.** Connect GitHub and Linear and Keel resolves `#4412` or `auth-service` inline. Search spans chat, PR discussion, and issue comments in one query. Median result time on a 200-person workspace: 380 ms.

**Interruption budgets.** Managers see per-person notification volume by week. Not a wellness dashboard — a number you can take into a 1:1 when someone's focus time has been under 2 hours a day for a month.

**Admin without a services contract.** SCIM, SAML, audit log export, and per-channel retention ship on every plan above Team. Self-hosted runs on a single Postgres instance.

**Migration.** Import Slack or Teams history with threads and permissions intact. Typical 100-person workspace: under an hour, and we do it with you on a call.

$8 per user/month. No enterprise tier that hides SSO behind a sales call.
