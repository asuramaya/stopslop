**We're moving to a new platform next quarter**

Starting next quarter, we're migrating this service to a new infrastructure platform. The change touches how we host and run things behind the scenes; it doesn't change what you use day to day.

Why now: our current provider can't keep pace with the traffic and reliability targets we've set for the next year. The new platform gives us faster response times, better uptime, and room to add features we've had queued up for months.

What to expect:

Your login, data, and existing integrations carry over. You won't need to re-enter credentials or reconfigure anything on your end.

There will be one maintenance window during the cutover, likely a few hours late at night in your local time zone. We'll send the exact date and time at least two weeks ahead.

API endpoints stay the same. If you call our API directly, no changes to URLs, auth, or request formats.

Support and billing continue without interruption. Your subscription, invoices, and support contacts stay as they are.

If you've built custom scripts or automation against our API, test them against our staging environment before the cutover. We'll open staging access two weeks in advance and post the exact date in your account dashboard.

Questions about the migration go to support@[company].com. We'll follow up with the maintenance window date as soon as it's confirmed.

Thanks for sticking with us through this. We built this platform to make the service faster and more reliable for you.
