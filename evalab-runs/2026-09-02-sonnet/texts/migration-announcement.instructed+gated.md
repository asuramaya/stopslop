Your service is moving to a new platform next quarter.

We're migrating to a new infrastructure platform starting in Q4. The move addresses three problems on the current system: slower response times during peak hours, a support process that requires too many manual steps, and a pricing structure that doesn't match how most customers actually use the product.

The migration happens in stages. We move accounts in batches over six weeks, starting with free-tier accounts in October and finishing with enterprise accounts by mid-November. You'll get a notice seven days before your batch moves, and another when it's done.

Your login, API keys, and existing integrations carry over. You won't need to reconfigure webhooks, re-issue tokens, or update SDK versions.

Accounts using the legacy export API (v1) need to switch to v2 before their migration date, since v1 won't run on the new platform. If that applies to you, we've already sent a separate email with the steps. During your migration window, also expect a maintenance period of up to 30 minutes where the service is read-only. We'll schedule this outside business hours for your time zone and confirm the exact time in your seven-day notice.

Pricing stays the same through the end of the year. Starting January, the new usage-based tiers take effect, and existing customers get the option to stay on their current plan for 90 days after that if the new tiers don't fit.

Questions about your specific migration date go to support@[company].com with your account ID.
