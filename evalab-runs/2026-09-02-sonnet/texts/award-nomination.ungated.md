Deploy Excellence Nomination: The Platform Team

Six months ago, shipping a release meant a two-hour manual checklist, a Slack thread full of "is it safe to deploy yet," and at least one rollback per sprint. The platform team fixed that.

They rebuilt our deployment pipeline from the ground up: automated canary rollouts, real-time health checks that halt bad deploys before they reach full traffic, and a rollback that now takes under a minute instead of twenty. Deploy frequency has roughly tripled, and our change-failure rate dropped by more than half. Just as important, the fear is gone — engineers ship on Friday afternoons again without a second thought.

What stands out is how they did it. Every change went out incrementally, with data behind it, and the team kept the rest of engineering in the loop the whole way through documentation, office hours, and a migration guide that made adoption painless for every other team. This wasn't a rewrite handed down from on high; it was built with constant feedback from the people who'd actually use it.

This team turned deployment from a source of dread into a non-event, and that shift touches every engineer who ships code here. They've earned this recognition for making the entire org faster and safer at the same time.
