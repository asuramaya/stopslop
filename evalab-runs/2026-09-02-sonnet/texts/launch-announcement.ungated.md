Dark Mode Is Here

You can turn the lights off now. Dark mode is live on every platform — desktop, web, and mobile — and it follows your system setting by default, or you can switch it manually from Settings > Appearance.

We rebuilt every screen for it, not just inverted the colors. Editor text, sidebar icons, tags, checkboxes, and the search overlay all got new contrast values so nothing reads as gray-on-gray at 2am. Syntax highlighting in code blocks got a separate palette tuned for a dark background, since the light-mode colors washed out badly when we just flipped them.

A few things worth knowing:

Images and embedded PDFs keep their original colors — we don't invert them, so screenshots and photos still look correct.

Themes sync across devices through your account, so switching on your laptop switches your phone too.

If you use a third-party plugin, some older ones still render with light-mode assumptions. We've flagged the ones we know about in the plugin directory and are working with those authors on fixes.

This took longer than we expected. Early versions had the editor cursor nearly invisible against the new background, and we caught a contrast problem in the tag picker that would have made it fail basic accessibility checks. Both are fixed in this release.

Turn it on from Settings, or let it follow your OS. Either way, tell us what looks wrong — there are surely a few corners we missed, and we'd rather hear about them now than in six months.
