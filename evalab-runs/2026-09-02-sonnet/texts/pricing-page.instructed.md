Pricing

Free
For solo developers and small side projects. Scan up to 500 lines per pull request, with the core rule set covering security, correctness, and style. Connects to one GitHub or GitLab repository. Community support through our forum.

Team — $18 per developer per month
For teams shipping on a shared codebase. Unlimited lines per pull request, unlimited repositories. Custom rule sets your team writes and maintains, plus inline comments on every PR. Slack and Linear integrations. Priority email support with a same-day response during business hours.

Enterprise — custom pricing
For organizations with compliance requirements or self-hosted infrastructure. Everything in Team, plus SSO, audit logs, and on-premise deployment. A dedicated account manager and a service-level agreement with guaranteed uptime. Custom onboarding for engineering teams above 50 developers.

All plans include unlimited pull request scans and a 14-day trial of Team features. No credit card required to start.

What counts as a developer seat? Anyone who opens a pull request in a connected repository during the billing month. Reviewers and approvers who don't author code aren't counted.

Switching plans takes effect at the next billing cycle. Downgrading from Team to Free removes custom rule sets and unlimited repository access; existing scan history stays available for 90 days.

Questions about which plan fits your team: email sales@[product].dev with your repository count and team size, and we'll recommend a tier within one business day.
