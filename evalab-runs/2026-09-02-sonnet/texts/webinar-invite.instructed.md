Join us for a live session on database performance tuning.

Slow queries cost money and patience, and most teams fix them by trial and error. This webinar walks through a repeatable method: how to read a query plan, spot missing or unused indexes, and find the lock contention that stalls writes under load.

Topics covered:

1. Reading EXPLAIN ANALYZE output without guessing
2. Index design for read-heavy and write-heavy tables
3. Connection pooling and when it backfires
4. Diagnosing lock contention during concurrent writes
5. When to denormalize, and when it's the wrong fix
