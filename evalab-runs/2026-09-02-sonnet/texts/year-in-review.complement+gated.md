This year the team shipped fewer things at once and finished more of them. The checkout redesign moved from three competing prototypes to one, launched in October, and cut cart abandonment by eleven percent. The migration off the old queue system took longer than planned, but it removed the class of timeout bugs that used to eat a Friday every month. We split the platform team from the product teams in March, which gave product engineers a clearer path to ship without waiting on shared infrastructure and gave the platform team room to fix the deploy pipeline properly instead of patching it between requests. Deploy time dropped from twenty-two minutes to six. Incidents dropped from eleven last year to four this year; two of those four came from the same root cause, a config drift issue in staging, which we closed with a validation step in the CI pipeline. Hiring was slower than we wanted. We brought on three engineers instead of the six we planned, and leaned on contractors to cover the gap on the mobile rewrite, which is about sixty percent done and will carry into next year. We also changed the postmortem process, moving from a shared doc to a short recorded walkthrough, and attendance at the follow-up review went up because people could watch it on their own time. Next year the priority is finishing the mobile rewrite and paying down the test coverage gap in the billing service, which is still under forty percent.
