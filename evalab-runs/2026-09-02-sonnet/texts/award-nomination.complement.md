I nominate the Platform Infrastructure team for their work overhauling our deployment process this quarter.

Six months ago, shipping to production meant a 45-minute manual checklist, a Slack thread of approvals, and at least one rollback a week. The team rebuilt the pipeline from the ground up: automated canary releases, pre-flight checks that catch config errors before they reach staging, and a rollback that now takes under two minutes instead of twenty.

The numbers tell most of the story. Deploy frequency is up from twice a week to multiple times a day. Failed deploys dropped by 80%. On-call pages related to bad releases are down to nearly zero. But the real win is what this freed up: engineers stopped scheduling their work around deploy windows and started shipping when a change was ready.

They also did this without a single day of forced downtime, migrating live traffic to the new pipeline in stages and writing clear documentation so every team could switch over without hand-holding.

This wasn't a side project. It was deliberate, well-tested infrastructure work that made every other team faster. That's the kind of impact this award should recognize.
