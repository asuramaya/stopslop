Senior Backend Engineer — Payments Platform

We move money for about 40,000 small businesses, and the systems behind that can't have a bad day. We're hiring a senior backend engineer to own core services in our payments infrastructure: ledger, settlement, and the APIs our partner banks integrate against.

You'll work mostly in Go and Postgres, with services running on Kubernetes. Expect to spend real time on idempotency, reconciliation, and failure modes — a duplicate charge or a missed settlement isn't a minor bug here, it's a support queue and a compliance conversation. You'll pair closely with our compliance and risk teams, since a lot of what we build has to satisfy auditors as well as users.

What you'll do:

1. Design and build services that handle transaction processing, ledger updates, and settlement with banking partners
2. Improve reliability and observability across systems already carrying production traffic
3. Review designs and code from other engineers, and help set technical direction for the payments team
4. Debug incidents that touch money movement, including ones that need coordination with external banking partners

What we're looking for:

1. 5+ years building backend systems in production, ideally including one with financial, regulatory, or other high-stakes correctness requirements
2. Strong SQL and data modeling skills — you should be comfortable reasoning about transactions, isolation levels, and race conditions
3. You should be able to reason through what happens when a call times out, when a message gets delivered twice, when two writes race
4. Clear written communication — a lot of coordination with compliance and partner banks happens in writing

Compensation is $170,000-$210,000 base plus equity, depending on experience. We're a team of 140, fully remote within the US, with quarterly in-person weeks.

To apply, send a resume and a short note about a production incident you handled to careers@[company].com.
