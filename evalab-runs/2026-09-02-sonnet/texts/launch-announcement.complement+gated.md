Introducing dark mode.

Enough of you asked. Starting today, every note screen in the app supports a dark theme: the editor, the sidebar, search, tags, everything. Open Settings > Appearance and pick Light, Dark, or System, and the app follows your OS setting automatically if you choose System.

We built this for two situations we heard about most: writing late at night without a bright screen in your face, and cutting battery drain on OLED phones, where a dark interface draws less power. Both were requests in our feedback inbox for months, and dark mode was consistently the top request in our roadmap poll.

A few details worth knowing. Syntax highlighting in code blocks now has its own dark palette, so contrast stays readable instead of just inverting colors. Exported PDFs still render on a white background regardless of your theme, since that's what most people expect when they share or print a note. And if you use custom note colors or highlights, we adjusted the palette so nothing washes out against the dark background.

The theme setting syncs across devices, so switching to Dark on your laptop switches your phone and tablet too, next time they connect.

This is the first version. Third-party markdown themes, embedded images with white backgrounds, and PDF exports are the places most likely to need fixes, and we'll ship follow-ups as we hear from you.
