Senior Backend Engineer

We move money for people who can't afford for it to break. Our platform processes payments for around 40,000 small businesses, and we're hiring a senior backend engineer to help rebuild the ledger service that sits underneath all of it.

You'll own a service end to end: the ledger that reconciles every transaction against the balances we report to customers and regulators. Today it's a monolith with a few known consistency bugs under high load. Your first project is fixing those bugs, then splitting the reconciliation logic into a service that scales independently.

We need someone with 6+ years of backend experience, including at least 2 years working with financial or other high-stakes transactional data. You should know your way around Postgres at scale — partitioning, replication, query plans under load. We use Go for most services, but we care more about your grasp of distributed systems and data consistency than about matching our stack exactly. You'll need to read and write SQL comfortably, reason about idempotency and retries, and explain a tradeoff to a compliance officer as clearly as to another engineer.

The team is nine engineers, two of them senior. You'd report to our VP of Engineering and work closely with the compliance and risk teams, since most of what you build has audit requirements attached.

Compensation is $175,000-$220,000 base, plus equity and a 10% target bonus. We offer full health coverage, a 401(k) match up to 4%, and unlimited PTO with a 15-day minimum we actually enforce. The role is hybrid, three days a week in our Denver office.

To apply, send a resume and a short note about a time you diagnosed a data consistency bug in production.
