A chat app built for teams that ship code, not teams that hold meetings.

Most chat tools optimize for volume: more channels, more reactions, more notifications. Your team already has enough of that. What's missing is a place where engineering conversations survive standup reminders and gif threads.

Threads resolve. Every conversation carries a status of open, blocked, or done, so nothing scrolls off unresolved and you see at a glance what still waits on someone.

The app renders code by default. Paste a stack trace or a diff and it shows up with syntax highlighting instead of a wall of unformatted text. Link a pull request and the thread tracks its status.

Notification budgets replace notification floods. Each person sets how much interruption they tolerate per hour, and the app enforces it — no more choosing between muting everything and drowning in pings.

The app keeps an audit trail. Decisions made in chat stay searchable and exportable, which matters six months later when someone asks why the team chose Postgres over Mongo and nobody remembers.

It connects to the tools you run. GitHub, Linear, PagerDuty, and your CI pipeline post updates as structured messages, not raw webhook JSON dumped into a channel.
