Welcome. You're joining a team that builds software people rely on every day, and today marks the start of figuring out how you fit into that.

Your first few weeks won't look like much output. You'll get your laptop set up, meet the people you'll work with, and spend real time just reading code and asking questions. That's expected. Nobody here remembers their first pull request being their best one.

A few things worth knowing right away:

**Ask questions in the open channels, not just in DMs.** Someone else on the team probably has the same question, and answering it publicly saves the next new hire from asking it again.

**We ship in small pieces.** Big rewrites are rare. If a change feels too large to review in one sitting, it's probably too large to merge in one sitting too.

**Code review is where we teach each other.** Expect comments on your first few PRs. They're not a judgment on you; they're how the codebase stays consistent as more people touch it.

**Your onboarding buddy is your first stop.** They've been assigned to you and will check in daily for the first two weeks. Use them liberally.

Your manager will walk you through your first 30/60/90 day plan this week. Until then, your only job is to get your environment running, read through the codebase, and start forming questions.

We're glad you're here. Go get your laptop set up, and come find your buddy when you're ready.
