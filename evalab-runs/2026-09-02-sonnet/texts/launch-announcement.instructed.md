Dark mode is here.

You can switch it on from Settings > Appearance, or let it follow your system clock. Every screen — the editor, the sidebar, search — now renders in a low-light palette we tuned by hand, not by inverting colors and calling it done.

The text color and contrast ratios meet WCAG AA, so long notes stay readable at 11pm. Code blocks, tables, and highlighted text keep their own dark-mode variants instead of washing out to gray.

Three theme options are available: Light, Dark, and Auto. Auto switches based on your OS setting, so a laptop set to dark at sunset switches your notes over with it. If you prefer a fixed choice, Light and Dark stay put regardless of OS or time of day.

This shipped because it was the most requested feature in our support inbox for the past six months, with over 2,000 votes on the feature board. We rebuilt the color system from scratch rather than patching the existing one, which is why it took this long.

The rollout is gradual: iOS and Android get it today, desktop and web follow next week. If you don't see the toggle yet, check back in a few days.

We're also opening the color values themselves. The full palette — background layers, text tones, accent colors — is documented on our developer site, so anyone building plugins or themes can match the system exactly instead of guessing at hex codes.
