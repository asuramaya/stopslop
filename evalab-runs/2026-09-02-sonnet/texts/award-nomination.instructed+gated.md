I nominate the Deployment Engineering team for this year's award.

Six months ago a release took most of a day and needed someone watching every step. Now a merge to main goes to production in under twenty minutes, and any engineer on the team can trigger it without asking for help.

Three people rebuilt the pipeline stage by stage while the rest of us kept shipping. The old system stayed up the whole time the new one came online. Rollback used to mean paging someone who knew the manual steps by heart; now it's one command, and it has saved us twice this quarter without anyone needing to escalate.

They wrote the runbook so the next person doesn't have to reverse-engineer their decisions, and they ran two brown-bag sessions to walk other teams through adopting the same pipeline. Platform and payments both asked to reuse this work and now run the same setup.

I've watched three separate incidents get contained in minutes instead of hours because of what this team built.
