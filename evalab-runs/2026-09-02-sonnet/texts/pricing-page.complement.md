Pricing

**Solo — $19/month**
For one developer working across personal and client repos. Connect unlimited private repositories, run automated review on every pull request, and get inline comments for bugs, security issues, and style violations. Includes 500 review credits a month, email support, and access to the standard rule set.

**Team — $49/user/month**
For teams that want consistent review standards across every repository. Everything in Solo, plus shared configuration so every project follows the same rules, custom rule sets your team writes and maintains, and a dashboard showing review trends by repo and author. Includes 2,000 review credits per user, Slack and GitHub integrations, and priority support with a same-day response.

**Enterprise — custom pricing**
For organizations that need control over where code goes and who can see it. Everything in Team, plus self-hosted deployment, SSO and SCIM provisioning, audit logs, and a dedicated account manager. Review credits scale with your usage; we work out a number with you before you sign. Contact sales for a quote.

All plans include a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. Cancel anytime — you keep access through the end of your billing period.

**Frequently asked**

*What's a review credit?* One credit covers one pull request review, regardless of size. Unused credits don't roll over.

*Can I switch plans?* Yes, upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing settings. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

*Do you offer discounts for open source?* Yes. Public repositories under an OSI-approved license get Team-tier features free. Apply from your account settings.
