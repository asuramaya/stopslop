Every quarter we promise to slow down and pay down debt, then something ships and the promise slides again. This issue is about the debt anyway — not the theoretical kind, but the specific stuff sitting in our codebase right now.

We're running three pieces this month. One engineer walks through the migration that finally killed the old auth middleware, and why it took four attempts to get right. Another looks at what happened when a team let a "temporary" caching layer run for two years — it worked, until it didn't, and the postmortem is worth your time. And we've got a shorter piece on how to talk about debt with a PM who just wants to know when the feature ships.

None of this is about guilt. Debt is a normal part of building things fast, and pretending otherwise just means it gets hidden instead of tracked. The teams that handle it well aren't the ones with zero debt — they're the ones who know exactly what they owe and why they took the loan.
