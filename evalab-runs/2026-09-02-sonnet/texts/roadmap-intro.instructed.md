This roadmap covers the next two quarters of work on the CLI, the API, and the editor integrations. We built it from three sources: support tickets tagged "feature request," the open issues with the most reactions on GitHub, and direct conversations with teams running us in CI.

Three themes run through it. First, faster feedback loops — cutting the time between a code change and a result you can act on, in the terminal and in the editor. Second, fewer surprises in CI — clearer failure output, more predictable retries, and less flakiness in shared runners. Third, better support for large monorepos, where our current defaults were tuned for smaller projects.

Dates on this page are targets, not commitments. We ship incrementally, and priorities shift when we learn something new from usage data or from you. If a date moves, we'll update it here rather than leave it stale.

Each item links to its tracking issue, where you can follow progress, see design discussion, and leave comments before we finalize behavior. If something you need isn't listed, open an issue — that's how most of the items on this page got here in the first place.
