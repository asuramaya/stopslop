Senior Backend Engineer

We're hiring a senior backend engineer to help build the payments infrastructure our customers depend on to move money reliably. You'll work on the systems that process transactions, reconcile ledgers, and connect to banking partners, with a direct hand in decisions about data consistency, fault tolerance, and API design.

What you'll do:

Design and build services that handle transaction processing and settlement at scale. Own the reliability of core payment flows, including monitoring, incident response, and postmortems. Work with product and compliance to translate regulatory requirements into system behavior. Mentor engineers earlier in their career and review code across the backend team. Participate in architecture decisions as we expand into new markets and payment rails.

What you'll need:

Five or more years building backend systems in production, with at least two years working on financial, payments, or other systems where correctness and auditability matter. Strong grasp of relational databases, transactional integrity, and idempotency in distributed systems. Experience with Go, Java, or a comparable statically typed language (our stack is primarily Go). Comfort working directly with external APIs and vendor integrations, including banking and card networks. Clear written communication, since a good chunk of the work happens in design docs and incident reviews.

Nice to have:

Experience with double-entry accounting systems or ledger design. Exposure to PCI DSS or SOC 2 compliance work. Background in a regulated industry.

We offer competitive salary and equity, full health coverage, a 401(k) match, and a hybrid schedule with two days a week in our downtown office. This role reports to the VP of Engineering and works closely with our compliance and product teams.

To apply, send your resume and a short note about a system you've built that had to get correctness right.
