Dark mode is here. Open Settings > Appearance and pick Dark, Light, or System, and the app matches your OS automatically if you choose the last one.

We built this because our top feature request for two years was relief from the white glare of the editor at night. The new theme dims the canvas, sidebar, and toolbar together, and we rewrote the syntax highlighting palette so code blocks and markdown symbols stay readable against the darker background instead of washing out.

A few details we cared about while building this. Contrast ratios meet WCAG AA across every text color in the app, including muted metadata like timestamps and tags. Images and embedded PDFs keep their original colors so your reference material doesn't get color-shifted. And the switch is instant: no flash of the old theme while pages reload, no flicker when you jump between notes.

Sync respects your choice per device. If you want dark mode on your laptop at night and light mode on your tablet during the day, set them independently; the app won't overwrite one with the other.

This is the first release of a larger visual overhaul. Next up: custom accent colors and adjustable editor contrast, both currently in testing with a small group of beta users.

If you hit a spot where dark mode looks off, a note background that didn't update or a plugin that clashes with the new palette, tell us through in-app feedback. We're tracking every report and fixing them in order of how many people hit them.

Try it under Settings > Appearance today.
