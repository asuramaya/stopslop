Your team already has Slack open in one tab and three other tools open in the rest. Relay is the one you can close the others for.

Every channel has an owner and an expiration date, set at creation, enforced automatically — no channels from a 2023 incident still pinging engineers who left the company. When a thread goes 14 days without a reply, Relay archives it and tells the creator why. Search returns code snippets with syntax highlighting intact, not screenshots of code, and pull request links unfurl with diff stats and review status, so you know if a link needs your attention before you click it.

Permissions are set at the team level once. New hires inherit the right channels the day they join, and no manager has to remember to add them to twelve places by hand.

Relay logs message edit history by default. When someone asks who said we'd ship Friday, the answer sits in the thread, not in someone's memory of a call from three weeks ago.

Pricing runs per active user, counted monthly, not per seat you provisioned in January and forgot to remove. Ask your finance team why the last three chat tool bills only went up. Relay's goes down when headcount does.

Migrate a Slack workspace in under a day. Bring the channel history, the integrations, the muscle memory. Leave the rest behind.
