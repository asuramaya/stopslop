**Senior Backend Engineer**

We're a mid-sized fintech company building payment infrastructure for small businesses. Our platform processes millions of transactions a day, and we're looking for a senior backend engineer to help us scale it.

You'll work on the systems that move money reliably: payment processing, ledger accuracy, and the APIs our partners depend on. You'll own projects from design through production, work closely with product and infrastructure teams, and mentor engineers earlier in their careers.

What you'll do: design and build backend services in Go or Python, with a focus on correctness and reliability. Improve the performance and scalability of our transaction and ledger systems. Work with PostgreSQL, Kafka, and AWS in a service-oriented architecture. Review code, debug production issues, and write clear technical documentation. Partner with compliance and security teams on systems that handle sensitive financial data.

What we're looking for: 5+ years building backend systems in production. A strong grasp of distributed systems, data consistency, and database design. Experience with high-throughput or financial systems helps but isn't required. Comfort owning a project end to end, including the tradeoffs. Clear written and verbal communication with both engineers and non-engineers.

We offer a competitive salary and equity, health, dental, and vision coverage from day one, a flexible remote work policy with quarterly in-person team weeks, and a 401(k) match with a real vacation policy.

We're a small team, so your work ships fast and matters. If you want to build the infrastructure behind how small businesses get paid, we'd like to talk. This role is remote in the US or hybrid out of our home office. Apply at careers@[company].com with your resume and a note about a system you're proud of building.
