**Senior Backend Engineer**
**Meridian Pay · Remote (US) · Full-time**

Meridian Pay builds the payment infrastructure banks and credit unions use to move money for their customers. We process a few million transactions a day, and the team behind that pipeline is still small enough that your code ships to production within your first week.

We're looking for a senior backend engineer to help us scale our core ledger and payments API. You'll work mostly in Go and Postgres, with some Kafka for event streaming between services. Recent projects on this team: rebuilding our idempotency layer after a partner integration exposed a race condition, and splitting a monolith payment service into three services without downtime.

**What you'll do**
- Design and build services that handle money movement, reconciliation, and settlement
- Own a piece of the system end to end — schema, API, monitoring, on-call
- Review PRs and mentor engineers earlier in their careers
- Work directly with our compliance and risk teams; fintech means the constraints are real, not theoretical

**What we're looking for**
- 5+ years building backend systems in production, ideally with a statically typed language
- Experience with relational databases at scale — you know why an index helps and why it sometimes doesn't
- Some exposure to financial systems, payments, or anything where correctness and auditability actually matter
- Comfortable being on call for a system that can't silently drop a transaction

**Compensation & benefits**
$160k–$210k base plus equity, full health coverage, 401k match, unlimited PTO (we mean it — the founders take it too).

Meridian Pay is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds.

To apply, send a resume and a short note about a system you've built that you're proud of to careers@meridianpay.example.
