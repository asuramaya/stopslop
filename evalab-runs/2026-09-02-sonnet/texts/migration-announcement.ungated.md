We are moving our service to a new platform next quarter.

The current infrastructure has run this service for several years and served you well, but it is reaching the limits of what it can support. The new platform gives us more room to grow, faster response times, and a foundation for the features you have asked for.

Here is what changes and what does not.

Your account, data, and settings carry over automatically. You do not need to re-enter anything or create a new login. The web address, API endpoints, and integrations you already use stay the same.

Behind the scenes, we are replacing the database and hosting layer that powers the service. Once the move is complete, you can expect faster page loads, fewer scheduled maintenance windows, and quicker fixes when something breaks.

We will migrate accounts in stages over several weeks rather than all at once, so we can catch problems early and fix them before they reach everyone. You will get an email a few days before your account moves, and another once it is done. During your migration window, the service may be briefly unavailable, typically for less than ten minutes.

If you use our API directly, check the migration guide we are publishing next month. It lists a small number of deprecated endpoints and their replacements, along with a timeline for the old ones to stop working.

Questions can go to support@[yourcompany].com. We will also hold two live Q&A sessions before the migration begins; dates will follow in a separate email.

Thank you for your patience while we make this change.
