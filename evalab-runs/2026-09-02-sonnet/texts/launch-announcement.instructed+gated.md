Dark mode is here.

Switch your notes to a dark interface from Settings > Appearance, or let the app follow your system theme automatically. The editor, sidebar, search, note list, code blocks, and embedded images all adjust.

We rebuilt the color system rather than inverting the existing palette. A straight invert washed out headers and made syntax highlighting unreadable in code blocks, so text stays close to true black or white, and accent colors are tuned separately for light and dark so links and highlights keep their contrast in both modes.

Three ways to set it. Light is the current look, on by default. Dark is the new theme. System switches with your OS setting, including scheduled switches if you've set your device to change at sunset.

The setting syncs across devices through your account, so switching it on your laptop carries over to your phone and tablet.

Dark mode was the most requested feature on our feedback board for the past six months. Writing at night in a bright white editor was the top complaint in our support tickets.

It's rolling out now to all users on version 4.2 and later. If you don't see it yet, check for an app update.
