Here's the roadmap here for the next two quarters.

We build tools for people who write code every day, so we owe you a clear account of where the product is going and why. This roadmap covers what we're shipping through the end of the year: faster incremental builds, a rewritten CLI, first-class support for monorepos, and a plugin API that lets you extend the editor without forking it.

Priorities came from three sources: support tickets, the public issue tracker, and conversations with teams running us in production. When those three disagreed, we picked the fix that removed the most friction from a normal Tuesday, not the one that demoed best.

Dates here are targets, not promises. Some items will slip. We would rather tell you a date is wrong two weeks before it lands than stay quiet and surprise you. If a milestone moves, we will update this page and say why.

This is a working document. Items move between "planned," "in progress," and "shipped" as the quarter goes on, and we drop things that stop making sense. If something you rely on is missing, file an issue. We read them, and they shape what shows up here next.
