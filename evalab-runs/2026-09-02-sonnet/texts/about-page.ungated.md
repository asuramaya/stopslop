**About Us**

Clinics don't run on good intentions. They run on the schedule — and when that breaks, everything downstream breaks with it: patients waiting rooms deep, staff double-booked, providers running two hours behind by lunch. We built our scheduling platform because we watched this happen at clinic after clinic, and the fix was never "try harder." It was better tools.

We started in 2019 with a simple observation: most scheduling software was built for salons and gyms, then awkwardly retrofitted for healthcare. Clinics need something different — support for multi-provider schedules, insurance verification tied to appointment types, recall reminders for chronic care patients, and rules that account for the fact that a 15-minute follow-up and a new-patient physical are not the same block of time.

Today, our platform handles scheduling for independent practices, multi-location clinic groups, and specialty offices across primary care, dermatology, physical therapy, and behavioral health. Front desk staff use it to fill gaps automatically when patients cancel. Practice managers use it to spot bottlenecks before they become backlogs. Patients use it to book, reschedule, and get reminders without a phone call.

We're a team of engineers, former practice administrators, and healthcare operations people. Several of us spent years working the front desk at clinics before we started writing code — we know what a bad no-show week does to a schedule, and we know what a good system feels like when it's working.

Our goal is not to add software to your clinic's day. It's to give you back the hours that a broken calendar quietly takes from you.
