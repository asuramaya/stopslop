About Us

Meridian Health builds scheduling software for medical clinics. We started after watching a friend's practice run three separate systems, one for booking, one for reminders, one for provider calendars, none of which talked to each other. Front desk staff spent their mornings reconciling double bookings by hand.

Our platform handles the whole scheduling flow: patient booking, provider availability, room and equipment assignment, and automated reminders that cut no-show rates. It works for a single-physician practice and for a multi-site clinic group with shared resources across locations. Everything syncs in real time, so a change made at the front desk shows up instantly on a provider's tablet. We built the system around a few constraints clinics told us mattered most: it has to work when the internet connection drops, so the local view keeps functioning and syncs once the connection returns; it has to meet HIPAA requirements without extra configuration, so encryption and audit logs are on by default; and it has to be usable by staff who have five minutes of training, not five hours.

The team includes former clinic administrators, schedulers, and engineers who spent years building systems for hospitals before deciding to focus on the smaller practices that hospital software ignores.

We test every feature with real front desk staff before it ships. A scheduling tool that confuses the person using it fails, regardless of what it can do on paper.

Today, clinics in over 30 states use Meridian Health to manage appointments for several million patients, and we keep building based on what staff tell us they need, one clinic at a time.
