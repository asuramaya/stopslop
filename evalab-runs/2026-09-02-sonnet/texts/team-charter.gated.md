Platform Engineering Team Charter

We build and operate the internal platform that lets product teams ship software without owning infrastructure. Our customers are engineers, not end users. We succeed when a team goes from code to production without asking us for help.

We own CI/CD pipelines, developer tooling, internal platforms (compute, networking, observability), and the paved-road standards teams follow by default. We do not own product code, on-call for product incidents, or business logic. Where we provide a self-service path, teams use it; where we don't, teams build their own and tell us what's missing.

Self-service comes first. Every capability we offer works without a ticket to us. We make the right path the easy path, and we don't block teams from stepping off it when they have a real reason — a paved road, not a mandate. Platform outages cascade across every team we serve, so we hold ourselves to a higher reliability bar than a typical product team. A tool nobody uses is a failure regardless of how well it's built, so we track adoption over activity.

We treat other engineering teams as customers with a feedback loop, not as requesters we route tickets to. We publish a roadmap, take input from teams building on the platform, and refuse work that doesn't move the platform forward. We document defaults so decisions don't live only in our heads.

We answer for deployment velocity, platform uptime, and the gap between what teams need and what self-service already covers. When that gap grows, closing it is our job before it becomes anyone else's problem.
