Dark mode is here

Starting today, you can switch your notes to a dark theme from Settings > Appearance, and it will follow your system preference automatically if you leave it on Auto.

We built this because it was the single most requested feature in our feedback inbox, by a wide margin. People write at night, on trains, in bed next to a sleeping partner, and a bright white screen doesn't fit those moments. The new theme uses a near-black background with warm gray text, tuned so long paragraphs stay readable without the glare.

A few details we spent real time on: syntax highlighting in code blocks now uses a separate dark palette instead of just inverting colors, checkboxes and tags keep their contrast ratios above WCAG AA, and images you've pasted into notes get a subtle border so they don't float awkwardly on black.

Switching is instant and doesn't touch your data. Your existing notes look the same, just with the colors flipped. If you sync across devices, the theme choice is local to each device, so you can run dark mode on your phone and light mode on your laptop if that's how you work.

This ships today on iOS, Android, and web. Desktop apps get it next week once we finish testing the print view, which still needs to render on white regardless of your theme.

Try it out and tell us what breaks. We read every piece of feedback that comes in, and dark mode itself came from that inbox.
