Senior Backend Engineer

We're a mid-sized fintech company building the payment infrastructure that banks and merchants rely on to move money safely. We're hiring a senior backend engineer to help scale our transaction processing systems as we expand into new markets.

What you'll do:

You'll own core services that handle payment authorization, settlement, and reconciliation. You'll design APIs consumed by both internal teams and external partners, and you'll work closely with our compliance team to make sure new features meet regulatory requirements before they ship. You'll also mentor mid-level engineers and take part in on-call rotation for the systems you build.

What we're looking for:

- 5+ years building backend systems in production, ideally with exposure to payments, banking, or another regulated industry
- Strong grasp of distributed systems fundamentals: consistency models, idempotency, retry logic, and failure handling in systems where a duplicate transaction is not an option
- Experience with PostgreSQL or a similar relational database at scale, including schema design for financial data
- Comfort working in Go or Java (our services run primarily in Go)
- A track record of writing code that other engineers can read, test, and maintain

Nice to have:

- Experience with double-entry ledger systems
- Familiarity with PCI DSS or SOC 2 compliance work
- Background integrating with card networks, ACH, or wire transfer rails

What we offer:

Competitive salary and equity, full health coverage, 401(k) match, and a remote-friendly policy with quarterly in-person team weeks. We're a team of about 80, so your work here has direct, visible impact on how the product handles real money for real customers.
