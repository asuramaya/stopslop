Free covers one repository, review on every pull request, and seven days of history — enough for a solo project or to try the tool before committing. No credit card required.

Team costs $18 per contributor per month and covers unlimited repositories, full review history, and custom rule sets per repository. Comment threads sync to GitHub and GitLab, reviews finish in under two minutes on a 500-line diff, and this tier adds Slack notifications plus a shared dashboard for open findings across repositories.

Enterprise is priced by seat count and support tier. Contact sales.

It includes SSO through SAML or OIDC, audit logs retained for one year, and a self-hosted deployment option for teams that cannot send code to a third party, plus a named support contact with a 4-hour first-response window for production issues.

All tiers run the same review engine.

The difference is retention, deployment location, and how many people can see the results. Most teams need review history past a week within the first month, which is what decides Free versus Team.

Every tier includes unlimited pull requests. We do not meter by lines reviewed or by number of comments, because that pricing model rewards us for finding problems that are not there.

Downgrading keeps your existing comment history for 30 days, then deletes it. Upgrading takes effect on the next billing cycle, and we prorate the difference for the days remaining in the current one.
