## On-call

The rotation is one week, handing over Monday morning. You'll shadow a week in your second week and take a real shift once you've shipped a change to production, usually around month two. Payments alerts page a human at any hour, because a stuck settlement batch gets worse the longer it sits. Sam keeps the runbooks in the wiki, and every alert links to one. If an alert fires without a usable runbook, that's a bug worth filing.
