## Local-first sync

Local-first sync is live on desktop, iOS, and Android as of today.

Until now, notes you wrote without a connection stayed on the device that made them. Open the app on your laptop after a flight and yesterday's scribbles were still on your phone.

Now every edit is written to a local log and replayed against the server when you reconnect. Edits to different paragraphs merge on their own. When two devices change the same lines, we keep both versions and show them side by side so you pick, instead of burying one in a duplicate file. Whichever device reconnects first lands its edits, and the second replays on top. A device that stays offline for a month is fine; the picker waits for you when it comes back.

The queue survives a force-quit and a reboot. There is nothing to turn on.

Bug reports go to support@example.com.
