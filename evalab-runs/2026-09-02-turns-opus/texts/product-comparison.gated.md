Use the batch run. One cron entry and a script you can read top to bottom is a thing one person holds in their head at 2am. A broker is a second production system with its own failure modes, its own dashboard nobody looks at, and its own upgrade path you will be doing on a Friday. Add a queue the first time a specific job has a person waiting on the other end of it, and add it for that job alone. Teams that reach for the queue early because it looks like the grown-up architecture end up with four workers, a Redis they can't lose, and one job that ever needed any of it. Make the batch run idempotent on the first day you write it. You will be glad you did either way.
