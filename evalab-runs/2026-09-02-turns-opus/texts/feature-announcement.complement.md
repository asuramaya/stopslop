Local-first sync is now live in version 3.2.

Editing a note without a connection used to mean your changes sat on that device until you reopened the app somewhere with signal. Now the app keeps a full local copy of your notebook and reconciles it the moment a connection returns. Write on a plane, on a subway, in a basement office with one bar.

Conflicts get handled per paragraph rather than per note. If you edit the third paragraph on your laptop and someone on your shared notebook edits the first, both edits survive. When two edits touch the same paragraph, you'll see both versions side by side with a device name and timestamp, and the note stays flagged in your sidebar until you resolve it. That holds when two of your own devices go offline at once: each replays its queued edits on reconnect, and the later one does not overwrite the earlier.

Sync status shows in the bottom left. Tap it to see which notes are waiting. The local copy is encrypted with the same key as your synced data, so an offline device holds nothing readable without your passcode.

Available today on iOS, Android, and desktop. Web support is in beta behind a settings toggle.
