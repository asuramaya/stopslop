Local-first sync is now live in version 3.2.

Until now, notes you wrote without a connection sat in a local queue and waited. If you opened the same note on your phone and your laptop before either reconnected, one version quietly won and the other went away. Several of you wrote to us about losing a paragraph that way.

The new sync engine keeps a full edit history on each device and merges changes character by character instead of picking a winner. Add a heading on your laptop on a plane, rewrite the opening line on your phone in the terminal, and you get both. Order of reconnection changes nothing. If two devices touched the same words, the note opens with both versions side by side and stays that way until you pick one.

Local-first sync works on all platforms, though the iOS and Android apps need version 3.2 or later to participate in a merge. Older versions fall back to the old behavior.

You don't need to turn anything on. Sync happens in the background when a connection returns, and notes edited on a device with no connection show a small clock icon until they land on the server.
