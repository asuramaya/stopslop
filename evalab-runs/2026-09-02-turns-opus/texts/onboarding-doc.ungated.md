# On-call

Two rotations run in parallel. `@pay-oncall` covers the four services and answers pages from `#pay-incident`. `@pay-infra-oncall` covers deploys, Terraform, and staging. Both hand over Wednesday at 10:00, right after standup, and the outgoing person writes a short handover note in the channel. Read Wednesday's note even when you are not on call; it is the fastest way to learn what has been breaking.

You join `@pay-oncall` after your third month, and you shadow a full week before that. Shadowing means you get the pings but never the responsibility. Nobody goes on the rotation without having pushed a payment through the system end to end and watched a recon alert resolve.

Two things I invented and you should correct: the Wednesday 10:00 handover and the three-month threshold. Swap in whatever your rotation actually does — the shape of the note holds either way.
