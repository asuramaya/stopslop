## What we're building next

This page is the real plan. Not a wishlist we wrote for a board deck, but the same list our engineers pull work from on Monday morning.

Three things to know before you read it. Dates here are estimates, and estimates slip, so when something moves we move it on this page and say why in the changelog. We won't quietly delete a card and hope nobody kept a screenshot. "Planned" means we've committed to it, while "Exploring" means we're still arguing about it internally and a good comment from you can change the outcome. More than a few items now in the Shipped column started as an Exploring card that someone argued us out of, then into something better.

The board also isn't everything we do. Security work, infrastructure migrations, and the unglamorous business of keeping the API up eat about a third of our capacity in any given quarter, and none of it appears here.

Comment on any card, or open an issue. We read all of it. We reply to less of it than we'd like, which is a staffing problem we're working on. If something you depend on isn't on the list, say so. That's why the list is public.

Two things to swap for your own facts: the "third of our capacity" figure, and the column names (`Planned` / `Exploring` / `Shipped`) if your board uses different ones. The specificity is doing most of the work here, so replace it with true numbers rather than dropping it.
