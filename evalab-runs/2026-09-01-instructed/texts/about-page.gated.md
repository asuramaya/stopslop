## Why Northgate exists

We started this company because a front-desk coordinator in Fresno showed us that moving one appointment took her eleven clicks.

Eleven. For a patient standing right there, coat still on, asking to come back next Thursday.

Northgate builds scheduling software for outpatient clinics — family practice, dermatology, physical therapy, the six-to-twelve-provider groups that don't have an IT department and never will. Our founders spent six years at a large EHR vendor, most of it on support calls with receptionists, and came away convinced that the people who suffer most from bad scheduling tools are the ones nobody asks.

So we ask. No feature ships until we've watched someone use it at a real front desk during a real Monday morning. That's slower than the alternative. It's also why our reschedule flow is two clicks, why the waitlist backfills a canceled slot without anyone opening a second screen, and why clinics on Northgate report no-show rates around 6% against a 12% national average.

We are deliberately narrow. We don't do billing. We don't do charting. We integrate with the systems that do, and we stay out of the way of your clinical staff.

Thirty-one people, most of them in Sacramento, a few scattered. Roughly a third have worked in a clinic themselves. We answer our own support line, and the person who picks up can change your templates while you're still talking.

If your schedule is the thing that keeps breaking your day, we'd like to hear about it.
