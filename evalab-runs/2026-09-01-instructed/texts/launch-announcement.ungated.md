**Dark mode is here. It took us three years.**

Margin 2.4 ships tonight with a dark theme on macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android. If you want it, it's under Settings → Appearance, with a "Match system" option that follows your OS after sunset.

The delay wasn't neglect. Margin renders notes on a paper texture, and paper is the whole point of the app: a faint grain, warm off-white, ink that sits slightly into the page. Inverting that gave us grey mush. Every attempt looked like a photocopy of a photocopy, so we shelved it twice.

What finally worked was giving up on inversion. We rebuilt the theme from the ink side out. The background is #16161A, a near-black with a little blue in it. Body text sits at #D8D6D1, which measures 12.4:1 against that background, well past the 7:1 that WCAG asks for at AAA. Highlights, code blocks, and the six tag colors were all repicked by hand rather than run through a filter.

Two things you should know. Images in notes are not dimmed by default, because photographers on the beta asked us not to touch their work; there's a toggle if you disagree. And if you use a custom theme from the plugin gallery, it will keep its light palette until its author updates it.

We're already reading the beta forum thread. If a color reads wrong on your display, post a screenshot there and tell us the panel you're on.
