# Roadmap

We keep getting asked what we're building next, usually in a GitHub issue that already has forty thumbs-up on it. So here's the list.

A few notes on how to read it.

Nothing here has a date. We tried shipping dates twice and missed both, and a missed date on a public page is worse than no date at all — it teaches people to discount everything else we say. Instead each item sits in one of three buckets: building now, next up, and thinking about it. Items move backward sometimes.

"Thinking about it" means exactly that. Several of those will die. When one does, we'll move it to the graveyard at the bottom of the page with a paragraph on why, because the reasoning is usually more useful to you than the feature would have been.

We don't list security patches, dependency upgrades, or the slow grind of getting CLI startup under 100ms. That's close to half of what we do in a quarter, and it doesn't fit the format.

If the thing you need isn't here, open an issue. We read all of them. That's where most of this came from.
