# Senior backend engineer

**Location:** Chicago or remote (US, UTC-4 to UTC-8)
**Compensation:** $185,000–$225,000 + equity

We process about 40 million payment authorizations a month for small-business lenders. The systems that do it are Go and Python services on AWS, backed by Postgres and Kafka, and some of them were written when we handled a tenth of that volume. We need someone senior enough to tell the difference between what needs rewriting and what just needs better instrumentation.

You would join the payments platform team: six engineers, one product manager, no separate QA. The team owns ledger correctness, authorization latency, and the reconciliation jobs that settle with our banking partners overnight. Most weeks involve a mix of feature work and paying down the parts of the ledger service that make on-call unpleasant.

What we are looking for:

1. Seven or more years writing backend services, including several on systems where data loss or double-charging had real consequences.
2. Fluency in Go or a willingness to learn it quickly. Python and SQL are unavoidable here.
3. Experience with distributed systems failure modes: idempotency, exactly-once delusions, partial writes, retry storms.
4. Comfort operating what you build. On-call is one week in six, and the pager is quiet most nights.

What we do not require: a computer science degree, fintech experience, or a LeetCode marathon. Our interview is a systems design conversation, a code review exercise on our actual codebase, and two conversations with the team.

We are regulated, which means audit trails, change control, and occasional paperwork. If that sounds intolerable, this is not the job.
