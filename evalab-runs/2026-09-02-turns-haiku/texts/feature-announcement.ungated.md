**Your notes work local-first now**

We've added local-first sync so you can write, edit, and organize your notes without an internet connection. When you reconnect, your changes sync automatically across all your devices.

The feature works on mobile and web. Open your notes offline, make changes, and they'll sync when you're back online. If you edit the same note on multiple devices while offline, we merge your changes—your most recent edits take priority.

You don't need to set anything up; it's on by default. Your notes stay local and encrypted on your device until sync, just like everything else in the app.

Useful for travel, commutes, or spotty connectivity. Sync completes within seconds for most people. Questions? Check our help docs or reach out to support.
