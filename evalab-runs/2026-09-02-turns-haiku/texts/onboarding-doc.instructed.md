## On-call rotation

You'll join the on-call rotation after your first month. The rotation runs weekly—each engineer covers production issues for one week at a time. During your week, you're the first responder for alerts and escalations. Check the #incidents channel in Discord immediately when something pages.

Most weeks are quiet. When issues do come up, pair with whoever's available—you're never alone debugging production. The team uses a runbook for common issues; keep it bookmarked.

Your on-call week is considered work time, not bonus time. If an incident runs late, take time off the next day. We track on-call load to keep rotation fair.
