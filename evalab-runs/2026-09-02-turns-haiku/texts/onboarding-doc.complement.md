## Who to ask

Your onboarding buddy and the tech lead handle architecture questions. The wiki has our architecture doc.

Message the on-call engineer for deployments and incidents. Discord posts the rotation during off-hours.

Database access and schema questions go to ops via a ticket (template pinned in #payments-ops).

Any senior engineer can explain payment processing logic and edge cases. The code is self-documenting, but find whoever's least busy.

When you need to know testing approach, pair with whoever wrote the test you're reading. Our standards are high for transaction-touching code — ask before you commit.

Discord #payments for everything else. Expect an answer within an hour during business hours.

## On-call rotation

The rotation runs one week at a time. Every engineer rotates in every four weeks. Discord shows who's on-call now and for the next month. During your first month, you won't be on-call — the team handles it while you ramp up. Read the incident post-mortems to understand what on-call looks like. When your turn comes, pair with whoever just finished their rotation the first day.
