Your notes with local-first sync

We've shipped local-first sync. Create, edit, and organize your notes whether you're connected to the internet or not. When you reconnect, your changes automatically merge with any edits made on other devices.

Your notes live locally on each device. When you're offline, changes queue in the background and sync to your account the moment you reconnect. If the same note was edited on two devices, the app shows you both versions and lets you pick which changes to keep.

No connection required. No waiting for servers. No lost work when the network drops. We built this because reliability matters more than features. Your work shouldn't vanish because of spotty wifi.

Try it the next time you lose connection.
