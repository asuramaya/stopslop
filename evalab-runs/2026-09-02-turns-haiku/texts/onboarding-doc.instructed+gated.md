You'll join the on-call rotation in week three, after you've shipped your first few changes. The rotation is weekly, Monday through Sunday. On-call means you're the first person called if a payment system fails—it's urgent work. You'll carry a pager and have access to production logs and dashboards. The rotation schedule is in the wiki calendar. During your on-call week, avoid scheduling vacation or major refactors. When you rotate off, the next person takes over at 9 AM Monday. The team pays on-call premium for weeks where you're on duty. You'll learn how to diagnose failures fast—this is where you figure out what actually breaks.
