**Your notes, everywhere: local-first sync is here**

We've added local-first sync to your note-taking app. Write, edit, and organize your notes whether you're online or not. Changes sync automatically when you reconnect.

**How it works**

Your notes stay on your device. When you work offline, the app tracks changes locally. Once you're back online, those changes sync to your account. If you edit the same note on multiple devices while offline, the app merges them intelligently—your latest content always wins.

**Why it matters**

No more waiting for a connection to save important thoughts. Take notes on a flight, in the subway, or anywhere without wifi. Your work is safe locally and syncs when you reconnect. Your encryption keys never leave your device.

**Available now**

Local-first sync rolls out today on iOS, Android, and desktop. Your notes are already syncing in the background. No setup required.
