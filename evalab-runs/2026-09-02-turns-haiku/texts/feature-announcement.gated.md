Your Notes Are Always Accessible—Everywhere With Local-First Sync

Your notes are now available everywhere, even without internet. We've rebuilt the app so it works just as well offline as online. When offline, you create, edit, and organize notes as usual. Everything stores locally, and once you reconnect, it syncs across all devices—no manual uploads, no merge conflicts, no waiting.

We kept this simple. You don't need to think about it.

If you edit a note on your phone offline, then edit the same note on your computer while still offline, both devices will sync when they reconnect. The app shows you both versions side by side so you can choose which to keep. Conflicts are rare, but when they happen, you stay in control.

This works on phones, tablets, and computers. Your full library is available offline on each device, or just the folders you choose.

Local-first sync is available now in version 4.2.
