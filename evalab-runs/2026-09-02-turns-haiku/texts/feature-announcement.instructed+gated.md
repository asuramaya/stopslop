Your notes stay in sync across all your devices, whether or not you have an internet connection. Edit from your phone, laptop, or tablet. Make changes, add notes, delete things — it all works offline.

When you reconnect, your changes sync automatically to all your devices. We send only what changed, not your entire note library. If your connection drops mid-sync, we resume automatically the next time you're back online.

When the same note is edited offline on multiple devices, both versions are preserved. You'll see both sets of changes side by side and can choose which to keep or combine them. This rarely happens — most people work on different notes across devices — but when it does, you have full visibility into what changed and where.

Local-first sync is on by default. No setup needed.
