Your notes, always available

We've released local-first sync. Write, edit, and organize your notes without an internet connection. When you're back online, your changes sync automatically across all your devices.

Start a note on your phone during your commute, finish it on your laptop at a coffee shop without internet, then find it updated on your tablet at home. Everything syncs automatically—no manual uploads or sync buttons.

Local-first sync keeps a copy of all your notes on your device. Changes queue up locally, then sync to our servers and your other devices when you reconnect. If you edit the same note on two devices while offline, the app shows you both versions and lets you choose which to keep.

The local copy makes the app faster—responses are instant because you're working with locally stored data.

Local-first sync is available now for all users. No setup required.
