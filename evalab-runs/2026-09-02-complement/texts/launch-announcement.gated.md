**Dark mode is here**

We got the request 4,300 times. It's the single most-asked-for thing in our feedback inbox, and it beat the runner-up (nested folders) by a factor of six. Dark mode ships today in Marginal 2.4, on desktop, iOS, and Android.

Turning it on takes one tap. Settings → Appearance, and pick Light, Dark, or Match System. If you pick the last one, your notes go dark when your phone does at sunset, and nothing else changes.

The part that took us longest wasn't the switch. It was the greys. Our first build used pure black behind white text, which looked sharp in screenshots and was miserable to actually read — the contrast ratio was around 21:1, and after ten minutes of writing your eyes start to ache from the halation around each letter. We settled on a near-black background at #16161A with text at 87% opacity. Boring numbers. Much better at 1 a.m.

Syntax highlighting in code blocks got its own palette rather than an inverted version of the light one, because inverted syntax colors turn muddy. Highlighter colors were re-tuned too. Your yellow highlights from three years ago will look yellow, not brown.

There's one gap we already know about. Images with transparent backgrounds and dark line art can disappear against the new canvas, and we're adding an automatic light backing plate for those in 2.5.
