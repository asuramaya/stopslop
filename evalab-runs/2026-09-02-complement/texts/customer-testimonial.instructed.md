## What teams say

"During an outage last March, we traced a checkout failure from the load balancer through four services in under ten minutes. Before, that would have meant SSHing into boxes and grepping." — Marcus Whitfield, SRE, Bellwether Commerce

"Honest note: the query language took our team about two weeks to get comfortable with. After that, people who had never touched logs before were building their own dashboards. Support handles a category of ticket now that used to escalate to engineering." — Dana Oyelaran, director of engineering, Kestrel Health

"We ingest roughly 900 GB a day across 200 services. Retention rules let us keep 30 days hot and a year in cold storage without a separate archival pipeline." — Tomás Beltrán, infrastructure architect, Vantablack Logistics

"The alerting is what we actually use it for. We wrote 60 rules in the first month and deleted 20 of them once we saw which ones were noise." — Ingrid Solheim, on-call rotation lead, Norbank
