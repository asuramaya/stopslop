Subject: Your account moves to the new platform in Q4

We are moving the reporting service to our new platform between October 6 and November 14. Your account will be migrated on one of those dates, and you will get a message two weeks before yours with the exact window.

API keys keep working, but the base URL changes; the v1 endpoints stay available until March 2027, so you have four months of overlap to update your integrations.

Expect one interruption. During your migration window, the service is read-only for up to three hours. Scheduled exports queued in that period run once the window closes. If a three-hour read-only period falls badly for your team, reply to this email and we will move your account to a different date.

Two things need action from you before your window opens. Confirm the admin contacts on your account are current, since those are the addresses we notify during the cutover. And if you allow traffic by IP, add the new ranges listed in the migration guide.

The guide is at docs.example.com/migration, and it covers the API changes endpoint by endpoint. Questions go to migration@example.com; we answer within one business day.

Thank you for bearing with the disruption. We will send a short confirmation once your account is through.
