Welcome. Glad you're here.

Your first week is deliberately slow. You will not ship anything critical, and nobody expects you to. Spend the first two days reading: the architecture notes in `docs/`, the last three months of design decisions, and whatever your onboarding buddy points you at. Ask questions in the open channel rather than in DMs, so the answers stay findable for the next person.

By Thursday you should have your environment running locally and one small change merged. It can be a typo fix. The point is to walk the whole path once, from branch to review to deploy, while the stakes are low.

A few things about how we work here.

Code review is where most of our teaching happens. Reviews are usually direct and sometimes long. That is not a judgment about you; it is the same treatment everyone gets, including the people who wrote the systems you are reading. If a review comment feels harsh, say so. We would rather recalibrate than have you quietly bracing for the next one.

We deploy several times a day. Outages happen. When one does, we write up what broke and why, and the write-up names systems rather than people. Nobody has ever been fired here for an outage.

You will find things that are wrong or half-finished. Some of them are known and tracked, some are not. Write them down as you go, because in three months you will have stopped noticing them, and that fresh view is genuinely useful to us right now.

Your buddy will find you this morning. Until then, coffee is on the second floor, and the wifi password is on the whiteboard by the stairs.
