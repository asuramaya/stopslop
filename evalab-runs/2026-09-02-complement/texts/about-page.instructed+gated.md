About Meridian Clinic Systems

We build scheduling software for outpatient medical clinics. That is the whole product. We do not do billing, we do not do charting, and we have turned down three acquisition offers from companies that wanted us to.

The company started in 2016 after our founder spent eleven months as a front-desk temp at a family practice in Sacramento. The clinic ran on a whiteboard and a decade-old calendar system that could not represent a shared provider, a room, and a piece of equipment as three separate constraints on one appointment. Double-bookings happened weekly.

Scheduling in a clinic is a constraint problem wearing a calendar's clothing. A dermatology follow-up needs fifteen minutes and any room. A Mohs procedure needs ninety minutes, a specific surgical suite, a histotech on site, and a surgeon who has not already been booked into a satellite office that morning. Most calendar software models the first case cleanly, then leaves the second one to the front desk, who holds the missing constraints in their head and catches the conflict on the morning of, or does not catch it at all. We model both.

Today Meridian handles appointment scheduling for 340 clinics across 22 states, ranging from two-provider practices to a 60-provider orthopedic group. Median no-show rate among our customers dropped from 11% to 7% in the first year after switching, mostly from the reminder logic rather than anything clever in the calendar itself.

We are 24 people. When you call support, the person who answers has scheduled patients for a living.
