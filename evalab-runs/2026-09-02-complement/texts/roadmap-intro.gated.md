Here is what we're working on, roughly in the order we expect to finish it. Three buckets. Things in progress have real engineers on them and a decent chance of landing this quarter. Things queued are agreed on but unstarted, which in our experience means the shape will change once someone opens the code. Things under consideration are arguments we're still having.

We publish this roadmap because the alternative is worse. When plans stay internal, you find out what we shipped only after we shipped it, and by then whatever workaround you built has become load-bearing.

Two caveats worth stating plainly. We don't put dates on individual items, because we've been wrong about dates often enough that publishing them just moves the disappointment earlier. And items move backward. A security issue, a dependency that breaks, a customer with a deadline we care about — any of these can push a queued item back to under consideration without much warning. When that happens we'll note it here rather than let the card quietly disappear.

If something you need isn't listed, say so in the issue tracker. We read it. Several items below started as a comment from someone who was annoyed about a missing feature and said so.
