## About Us

We build scheduling software for medical clinics. That's the whole product line, and it has been since 2016.

The company started because our founder, a former practice manager at a three-location family medicine group in Ohio, spent most of her week doing arithmetic that software should have done for her. Which provider had credentials for which insurance panel. Which room had the ultrasound cart. Which of the four patients who called Monday could actually be moved into Thursday's cancellation without breaking a referral requirement. She wrote the first version of our rules engine on a legal pad.

Clinic scheduling is harder than it looks from outside. A conference room booking has one constraint. A pediatric follow-up has fifteen, and half of them come from payer contracts rather than from the calendar. We spent the first two years mostly listening to front desk staff describe their workarounds, then built software that made the workarounds unnecessary.

Today around 900 clinics use our platform, most of them independent practices between two and forty providers. We integrate with the major EHR systems, we are SOC 2 Type II certified, and our support team answers the phone during clinic hours in every US time zone.

We are 64 people, remote across 19 states, and profitable. We have taken no outside funding, which means we answer to the clinics paying us rather than to a board looking for an exit. When a customer asks for something, the person who decides whether we build it is on the call.
