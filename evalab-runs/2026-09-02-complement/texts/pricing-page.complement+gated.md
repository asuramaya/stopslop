# Pricing

Free for one developer. Paid when your team needs shared standards and a record of what was found.

## Solo ($0)

Review your own commits on one repository, public or private. You get the full check set, inline comments on every pull request, and 30 days of history. No card required, and no seat count to manage. The cap is one seat and one repository, and when you pass it you can upgrade without moving anything.

## Team ($18 per developer, monthly)

Everything in Solo, across every repository you own. Shared rule sets mean two reviewers hold the same line.

Findings stay for a year, long enough to show whether the same class of bug keeps coming back. Merge protection holds a branch while a high-severity finding is open, and notifications go to Slack or email. We bill per developer who actually pushed that month, so a contractor between projects costs nothing.

## Enterprise (talk to us)

For teams whose compliance requirements the other tiers can't satisfy. Runners live inside your VPC, so source never leaves your network.

You get SAML sign-on, SCIM provisioning, and audit logs you can export to your SIEM. A named support engineer answers within four hours. Custom checks are written against your internal style guide, and we maintain them as it changes.

## Details

Annual billing takes 15% off Team.

Cancel from the dashboard and billing stops at the end of the period; nothing renews silently. Public repositories with an OSI-approved license get Team features at no cost, no application required.
