This month, the debt issue

Nobody schedules technical debt. It shows up anyway, usually on the Tuesday you promised something else.

We picked the theme because of a conversation that kept repeating in different rooms. Someone proposes a cleanup. Someone else asks what it buys us. And the honest answer — "I don't know yet, but the last four incidents all touched that file" — never wins the argument, because it sounds like a feeling instead of a case.

So this issue is about making the case. We have a piece on how the payments team paid down [X] years of accumulated workarounds without a rewrite, and what it cost them in feature velocity while they did it. A shorter one on the debt we're choosing to keep, and why that's a legitimate answer. Notes from the migration that went sideways in [month].

One thing worth saying up front. Debt isn't a moral failure, and the teams shipping the fastest are usually carrying plenty of it deliberately. The problem is the debt nobody decided on — the kind that accrued while everyone was busy, that now nobody owns, and that only surfaces at 3am.

Write the decision down. That's most of the work.
