**Senior Backend Engineer**
Remote (US) or Chicago office · $180–215k + equity

We move money. About 40,000 small businesses use our platform to run payroll, and every one of those transactions passes through services our backend team owns. When something breaks at 4am on the 15th of the month, real people don't get paid. That shapes how we build.

You'd join a team of nine engineers working on the ledger and payments core — double-entry accounting, ACH and RTP rails, reconciliation against bank partners. Most of the stack is Go and Postgres, with some older Python services we're gradually retiring. We run on AWS, deploy several times a day, and keep our own on-call rotation (roughly one week in six, and it's usually quiet).

What we're looking for:

- Five or more years building and operating backend services in production
- Real experience with relational databases under load — you've debugged a lock contention problem, not just read about one
- Comfort with correctness-critical work where retries, idempotency, and audit trails matter
- Willingness to talk to compliance and support, because they know things you don't

Fintech background is a plus, not a requirement. Several of our best people came from logistics and healthcare.

We're 300 people, Series C, profitable on a unit basis. Benefits are the usual good ones: full medical, 401k match, four weeks off that we actually expect you to use.

Our process is a 45-minute intro call, a take-home you can finish in three hours (paid), and a half-day onsite or virtual loop. We aim to give a decision within two weeks.
