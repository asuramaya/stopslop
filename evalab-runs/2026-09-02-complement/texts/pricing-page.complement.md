# Pricing

Every plan includes the full review engine. The tiers differ in how many repositories you connect and how much control you get over the rules.

## Solo — $12/month

One developer, unlimited private repositories. Reviews arrive as pull request comments within about 90 seconds of a push. You get the standard rule set: correctness bugs, unhandled errors, dead code, and dependency warnings. Configuration lives in a single YAML file you can commit. No seat management, no admin console, nothing to set up beyond installing the app.

## Team — $19 per developer/month

Everything in Solo, plus shared configuration across repositories. Write a rule once and apply it to all fifty of your services. Team adds review history, so you can see which rules fire most often and which ones your developers keep suppressing. You also get required-review gates: mark a rule as blocking and the pull request cannot merge until someone resolves the finding. Billing is per active developer, counted by commits in the last 30 days, so a contractor who stops pushing stops costing you money.

## Enterprise — from $8,000/year

Everything in Team, plus self-hosted deployment on your own infrastructure. Your code never leaves your network. Enterprise includes SAML single sign-on, audit logs with 7-year retention, and a custom rule API for the checks your compliance team already wrote. You get a named engineer for onboarding and a 4-hour support response commitment.

All plans include a 14-day trial with no card required. Annual billing saves two months. Open source projects use Team at no cost; email us with a link to the repository.
