Dark Mode ships today

You asked for this for two years. It landed in version 4.2 this morning.

Open Settings, pick Dark, and your notes move to a near-black background with off-white text. Choose System instead and the app follows your machine, so it flips at sunset along with the rest of your desktop.

We spent longer on this than we planned. Pure black looked sharp in screenshots and awful at 2am, so we lifted the background to #16161A and pulled the text down to #E4E4E7. Mira ran eleven color pairs through a contrast checker and cut the eight that failed WCAG AA. Code blocks got a separate palette, since syntax highlighting built for white backgrounds turns to mud once you invert it.

Images gave us the most trouble. We don't dim your photos or bake a filter over them. A screenshot you pasted last March looks like the screenshot you pasted last March. Diagrams saved with transparent backgrounds used to disappear into the page; we now paint a pale card behind them so the lines stay visible.

Two rough edges remain. PDF previews render on white, and a shared link opens in light mode for whoever you send it to. Devin is rebuilding the preview pane this month, and the sharing fix goes out with 4.3.

Update from the App Store, or restart the desktop app and it will pull the build on its own. If the contrast bothers your eyes on your monitor, mail support@ and tell us which display you use. Mira reads those and has changed the palette twice already because of them.
