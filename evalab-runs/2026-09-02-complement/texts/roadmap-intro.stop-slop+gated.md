This page shows the work we have committed to for the next two quarters, and the work we have turned down. We update it the first Monday of every month.

We publish it because customers asked us to. Three teams told us last year they had built workarounds for a feature we shipped six weeks later. Two more waited on a fix we had dropped without telling them, and found out only when they filed a second ticket asking where it had gone. You should be able to plan against our decisions instead of guessing at them.

The board has three columns. "Shipped" means the code runs in production and you can use it today. "Building" means an engineer is assigned and we expect the work to land this quarter. "Considering" means we are reading your issues and talking to customers, with no promise attached.

Dates slip. When ours do, we move the card and write a note explaining what changed. We will not delete an item and hope nobody noticed. If a card sits in Building past its quarter, the note tells you whether an engineer is still on it or we have pulled them onto something else, and we would rather write that note than let you discover the delay yourself.

You can comment on any card. Our product team reads the comments each Thursday and answers the ones that describe a specific use case. Start in the Building column.
