Welcome to your first day.

The first thing to know: nobody expects you to ship anything this week. Your onboarding buddy has blocked out time on their calendar for you through Friday, and you should use all of it. Ask the questions that feel too basic to ask. Everyone here has been three months in and still unsure what half the services do.

Your laptop should already be set up. If it isn't, message #it-help and someone will pick it up within the hour. The repo you'll spend most of your time in is called `platform-core`, and the README in it is honest about what's broken — we keep it that way on purpose.

A few things about how we work. We deploy on Tuesdays and Thursdays, never on Fridays. Code review is required, and reviewers are expected to respond within one business day; if yours goes quiet, ping them directly rather than waiting. We write design docs for anything that touches more than one service, and we keep them short.

You will break something eventually. When that happens, we write a blameless postmortem, fix the gap in the system, and move on. We have never fired anyone for an outage.

Your first task is in the ticket queue tagged `good-first-issue`. Pick whichever one looks interesting. The goal is to get a change through the full pipeline once, from branch to production, so the process stops being mysterious.

Lunch is at noon. Someone will come find you.
