# Pricing

## Solo — $12/month per developer

One reviewer for your own repositories. Unlimited private repos, review on every push, and comments that land in the pull request within about 90 seconds. Language support covers Python, TypeScript, Go, Ruby, Rust, and Java. You keep the full review history for 90 days.

## Team — $29/month per developer

Everything in Solo, plus the parts that only matter once more than one person touches the code. You can write custom rules in plain English ("flag any new endpoint without a rate limit") and the reviewer applies them across the whole codebase. Shared review policies mean a rule written by your staff engineer is enforced on everyone's branches, not just theirs. Team adds seat management through your existing SSO, a merge gate that can block on unresolved findings, and full history retention.

Most teams between 5 and 40 engineers land here.

## Enterprise — from $18,000/year

Self-hosted or in your VPC, so source code never leaves your network. Enterprise includes SAML, SCIM provisioning, audit logs exportable to your SIEM, and a support agreement with a four-hour response for outages. Pricing scales with seat count and starts at 100 seats. We quote annually, and if you are switching from another vendor we will meet you at your renewal date rather than asking you to pay twice for the same quarter.

## Details worth knowing

Every plan starts with a 14-day trial, no credit card until you convert.

Billing is per active developer per month. We count anyone who opened a pull request during the billing period, so people on leave or on other projects cost you nothing.

Open source repos are free on any tier. Email support@ with a link and we will turn it on.
