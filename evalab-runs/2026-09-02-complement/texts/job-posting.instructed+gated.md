## Senior backend engineer

Meridian Pay, Chicago or remote (US time zones). $185k–$215k, equity, full benefits.

We move money for about 4,000 small businesses — payroll runs, vendor payments, and the reconciliation work that sits underneath both. Roughly $9B settled last year across a platform that started as a Rails monolith in 2019 and is now a mix of that monolith and eleven Go services. You would own two of them.

The work is less glamorous than most fintech postings admit. You will spend real time on idempotency keys, on ACH return codes, on why a ledger entry posted twice during a partial network failure at 2am. Our on-call rotation is one week in six, and the person who broke something is the person who writes the postmortem.

What we need from you:

1. Seven or more years writing backend services, at least two in a system where being wrong costs money.
2. Deep Postgres knowledge. Not ORM familiarity — you can read a query plan and know when a transaction isolation level is the actual bug.
3. Go, or the demonstrated ability to learn it in a month. Several of our strongest engineers arrived from Java and Python.
4. Comfort with ambiguity in requirements from our compliance and treasury teams, who know what they need but not always how to describe it in a ticket.

We also like to see exposure to card networks, double-entry accounting systems, or SOC 2 audits, though none of it is required.

Our process is four steps: a 45-minute call with the hiring manager, a take-home you can finish in three hours (we pay $400 for it), a system design conversation, and a half-day with the team. Two weeks start to finish. We tell you no quickly if the answer is no.
