# Welcome

You start today. Someone on your team cloned the repo layout onto your laptop last week, so open a terminal and run `make bootstrap`. It takes about forty minutes. Get coffee. While that runs, read this.

We ship small changes often. Most engineers here merge something in their first three days, usually a typo fix or a missing test, and your onboarding buddy will point you at one. Nobody expects it to be impressive.

Code review is the main way we talk about code. Two approvals for anything touching billing or auth, one for everything else. Reviewers respond within a day. If yours doesn't, ping them. We would rather you nag than sit blocked, and none of us keeps score on how many times you asked.

You will break production at some point. When it happens, say so in #incidents immediately, then fix it. We write postmortems that name causes, not people. The engineer who took checkout down for eleven minutes in March wrote ours and still works here.

Ask questions in public channels. A question you post in #eng helps the next person who searches for it; a question you DM helps only you.

Spend your first week pairing with someone every afternoon, and sit in on the Tuesday architecture review even though none of it will make sense. Take notes on what confuses you and hand that list to your manager on Friday. That list is the most useful document you will produce this month, because the rest of us stopped noticing what's confusing years ago.

Laptop problems go to #it-help. Payroll and benefits go to Dana. Everything else, start with your buddy. Glad you're here.
