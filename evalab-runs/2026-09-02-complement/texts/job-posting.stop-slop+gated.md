**Senior Backend Engineer**
Meridian Pay — Chicago or remote (US)

We move about $4B a year in payouts for marketplaces and payroll platforms. Twelve engineers, four on backend. You would be the fifth.

What you would work on

Our ledger service handles roughly 900 transactions per second at peak and has to reconcile to the cent against six banking partners. Most of it is Go, some Python for the reconciliation batch jobs, Postgres underneath, all of it on AWS. Right now the reconciliation job takes eleven hours and blocks the Monday close. Fixing that is the first thing we'd hand you.

Then you'd split the ledger's write path so a slow partner API stops backing up the queue, and build the dispute workflow our operations team has been asking for since March.

What we need

Six or more years writing backend services that handle money, or something with similar failure costs. You have debugged a distributed system at 3am and can explain what you changed afterward. You know Postgres well enough to recognize a serializable isolation failure in production. Go experience helps, though we have hired people who learned it here.

You do not need fintech background. You do need patience for compliance reviews, because SOC 2 and state money transmitter rules shape a lot of what we build.

Details

$185,000 to $215,000, plus equity. Four days of PTO minimum per quarter, and we check. Two days in the office if you are local, otherwise fully remote in US timezones.

You'd have a 45-minute call with our VP of Engineering, then a debugging session on a real (sanitized) incident, then a conversation with two people you'd work beside. Three weeks start to finish.

Send a note to careers@meridianpay.com. Tell us about a system you fixed.
