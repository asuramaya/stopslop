# Pricing

Three plans. You pay for the people you add, not the lines they push.

Solo, $0

One seat, public repos without a cap, three private ones. You run the same review engine the paid plans use: inline comments on your pull requests within two minutes of a push, across eleven languages. No card. We keep it free because you tell your team about us.

Team, $18 per seat per month

Private repos without a cap. You write the rules the reviewer enforces, from naming conventions to per-directory coverage floors. Your engineers reply to a comment and the reviewer answers back in the thread. Push a shared rule pack and the rest of your repos pick it up on the next open PR. Slack and Linear hooks, SSO through Google or Okta, and 90 days of searchable review history come with it. Pay monthly, or take two months off the annual price.

Enterprise, from $12,000 a year

Run the reviewer inside your network. We ship a container that talks to nothing outside your VPC, so your source stays where your security team put it. You get audit logs and a support engineer who replies within four business hours. Ask for our SOC 2 Type II report and we send it before you sign anything.

Switch plans from the billing page and we refund the unused part of the month. Cancel and we keep your review history for 30 days in case you come back, then we delete it.
