We hired two developer advocates in January. A third starts in March.

I spent last November reading support tickets. Four hundred of them named the same thing, our OAuth flow. People got a token, hit a 401 on the next call, and gave up. Our docs covered the happy path in nine lines and skipped refresh. The API team last edited that page in 2023. That is the work I want these three doing. They will rewrite the page, then sit with someone who has never touched our SDK and watch them fail at it.

Sales asked why the money isn't going into ads.

Ads bring developers to the signup page. They don't get anyone past the 401. Last year we counted 11,400 signups and 2,300 first successful API calls. The other 9,100 people evaluated us and left, and we paid to bring most of them in. Three hires plus a conference budget runs us $600k a year. Move first-call conversion from 20% to 30% and we get another thousand working integrations. Finance puts each one above $600 in the first two years, so the math holds if we hit half the target.

I don't expect clean attribution. Someone reads a post in June, signs in November, and no report we run will link those two events. We will judge this on first-call conversion and on whether the OAuth tickets stop arriving.

If the queue looks the same next November, I was wrong and we'll say so.
