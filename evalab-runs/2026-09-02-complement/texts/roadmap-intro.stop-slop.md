We publish this roadmap so you can plan your own work against ours.

The board below shows what our engineers have open right now and what they pick up next. Each card names the person who owns it and links to the GitHub issue where the design argument happened. Read that thread if you want to know why we chose one API shape over another.

Dates move. We edit the card and post a comment about what got in the way. Subscribe to a card and you get that comment by email. We would rather tell you about a two-month delay in March than surprise you in May.

Nothing here is a contract. We cut features that stop making sense once we build a prototype, and we mark those cards as dropped instead of deleting them, so you can see what we walked away from and our reasoning.

Two things stay off the board: security fixes and work a customer asked us to keep quiet. The rest of what our team builds shows up here within a week of an engineer starting it.

Comment on a card if we got the priority wrong. We read those comments on Mondays, and we do reorder the board.
