# Pricing

Each plan runs the same review. You pay for seats and for control over where it runs.

## Solo, $12/month

One seat, wired to your personal GitHub or GitLab account. The reviewer comments on a pull request about ninety seconds after you open it. You get 300 diffs a month, enough for someone shipping a few times a day. Your rules live in a file in the repo, so you edit them the way you edit code. Cancel on the billing page and we stop charging you that day.

## Team, $29 per seat/month

Buy seats for people who open or review pull requests. Bots and read-only accounts cost nothing. Unlimited diffs, one rule set shared across your repos, and a dashboard that separates the findings your engineers acted on from the ones they closed untouched. Read that second column and delete the rules your team keeps dismissing. We answer support mail within one business day.

## Self-hosted, from $2,400/month

Run the reviewer in your own VPC. Your source stays inside your network. We ship a signed container image and you pick the week you upgrade. SSO through Okta or Entra, audit logs you can export to your SIEM, and an engineer on our side who joins your rollout calls by name. Above 200 engineers, write to us and we will quote the volume.

Every plan opens with a 14-day trial. We ask for a card when you invite your second teammate, not before. Move between Solo and Team whenever you want; we prorate the difference on your next invoice.
