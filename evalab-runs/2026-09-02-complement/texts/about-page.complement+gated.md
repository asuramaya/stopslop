# About Us

We started Corvid Health in 2017 after spending three years building intake software for a hospital network in Ohio. The clinics we worked with had the same complaint every time: the schedule was the thing everyone fought about. Front desk staff kept a paper backup because the system lied to them. Physicians found out about double-bookings at 8:05 in the morning. Nobody trusted the screen.

So we built a scheduler that tells the truth about what a clinic day actually looks like. Rooms, equipment, provider credentials, and interpreter availability all get treated as real constraints, because they are. When a patient books a 40-minute visit that needs an ultrasound room and a Spanish-speaking provider, our software either finds that slot or says plainly that it does not exist.

Today we serve 340 clinics across 18 states, mostly independent practices and community health centers between four and sixty providers.

We are 31 people, about half of us in Columbus and the rest scattered. Eleven of our staff worked in a clinic before they worked here, which is why our support line is answered by someone who knows what a no-show rate does to a Tuesday. We charge per provider per month. No implementation fee, no per-message billing, no separate line item for the patient reminders that make the whole thing work. Clinics can export their full schedule history at any time, in a format they can read without us.

We are privately held and profitable. We plan to stay both.
