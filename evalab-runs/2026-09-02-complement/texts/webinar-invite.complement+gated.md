Your queries are slow. Come find out why.

Live webinar on database performance tuning in practice
Thursday, September 18, 1:00–2:00 PM ET

Most teams meet their database at the worst possible moment. A dashboard turns red, a customer complains, and someone starts adding indexes to see what happens. This session covers what to do instead.

We walk through three real production slowdowns, from the first alert to the fix. You will see the query plans, the wrong guesses we made along the way, and the numbers before and after. Topics include reading an execution plan without memorizing the syntax, deciding when an index helps and when it costs more than it returns, spotting lock contention in connection pool metrics, and knowing when the problem is the query versus the schema underneath it.

Bring your own slow query. We reserve the last fifteen minutes for live diagnosis, and we take submissions in advance through the registration form.

Led by Priya Raghunathan, who has spent eleven years tuning Postgres and MySQL at companies from twelve people to twelve thousand.

Free to attend. Recording sent to everyone who registers, whether or not you make it live.
