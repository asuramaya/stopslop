Dark mode ships today in version 3.4.

You write at night. We watched the same request pile up in our support queue for two years, and we kept promising to get to it. Now it is there. Open Settings, tap Appearance, pick Dark. The app follows your system setting by default, so when your phone turns dark at sunset, your notes turn with it. Pin it to Light or Dark if you would rather decide once.

We built the palette around ink instead of black. Pure black makes white text vibrate on an OLED screen, and half our testers had a headache within the hour.

Our background sits at #16161A, body text at 88 percent white. Code blocks keep their syntax colors, but we pulled the saturation down so a Python file at 1 a.m. stops glowing. Two warnings before you switch. Images with transparent backgrounds can look strange against the dark canvas, so we added a toggle that puts a light card behind them. Your exported PDFs stay light, because you don't want a black page coming out of the printer.

Sarah on our design team spent six weeks on the highlight colors. Yellow over dark gray read like a warning label until she moved it toward amber and dropped the opacity to 30 percent. Ninety-one revisions for a highlighter.
