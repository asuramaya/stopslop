If you have a workflow you're worried about, tell us now rather than after. Write to [email] or reply here, and we'll check it against the new platform before the cutover and tell you what we find.

— [Name], [Role]

That's about 240 words, and two things are worth flagging. I've assumed a move where data and accounts travel with the customer, since that's the common case. If people must re-register or re-enter payment details, that fact belongs in the first paragraph rather than buried in the checklist. And the second paragraph only works if you fill in real pain points customers already feel. Generic reasons there, like "better performance," read as corporate throat-clearing and will cost you more trust than saying nothing.
