# Roadmap

We publish this roadmap because we get asked the same question in support tickets every week: is the thing I need being worked on, or should I build around it?

The answer is here, in three sections. Shipping covers work that is in progress and has a target quarter. Committed covers work we have decided to do but have not scheduled; treat the ordering as rough. Considering is a list of problems we think are real and have no plan for yet, which is where your comments change things fastest.

Dates slip. Ours have slipped by a quarter or more on four of the last twelve items, usually because a dependency turned out to be someone else's rewrite. When something moves, we edit the entry and note the reason rather than quietly changing the date.

Two things you will not find here. We leave off security work until it ships, for reasons that should be obvious. And we leave off anything that depends on a partner announcement we don't control.

Each entry links to its GitHub issue. That issue is the real artifact: the discussion, the design sketch, the objections from people who tried the beta. This page is an index over those issues, updated on the first Monday of each month.
