Four of us flew to Dublin for SREcon and split up to cover more of the schedule. We compared notes over dinner each night.

Priya sat in the incident review track and came back with a change we adopted the following Monday. The Monzo engineers write the full timeline before anyone proposes a cause. We had been doing the reverse, and our last three postmortems show it. Two of them name a root cause in the opening paragraph, then spend four pages defending it.

Dev spent Tuesday in the platform sessions, where two teams described building an internal developer platform before anyone asked for one and then maintaining a wrapper their own developers routed around. Shopify's staff engineer walked through tearing hers down. We have a wrapper like that. Dev is drafting a proposal to delete about half of it.

I went to the unglamorous talks about on-call. An engineer from Datadog described her team's paging budget: past two pages in a week, the team stops feature work until the pages stop. She said the rule survived three reorgs because someone put it in the roadmap template instead of a wiki page. Our on-call policy lives in a wiki page.

We listed eleven ideas on the flight home and picked three to try before October. Priya's timeline rule is running now. The wrapper deletion waits on Marco, who returns on the fifteenth. We have not raised the paging budget with anyone, since our current page rate would blow through it in the first week.
