# Welcome

You're here. Your accounts are already made, and the sticker drawer is by the window.

Today is deliberately light. Read this, set up your laptop, go to lunch with your team at noon. That's the whole agenda.

Your first week has one goal — ship something small to production. A copy fix, a log line, a flaky test, anything. Rana on Platform keeps a list of starter tickets and will pair with you on the deploy. People who wait until they understand the whole system before touching it tend to wait a long time. The system is large. Nobody understands all of it.

A few things that aren't written down anywhere else:

Ask questions in the public channel rather than in DMs. The answer usually helps someone who hasn't gotten around to asking yet.

We review code slowly and merge quickly. Expect comments. They're about the diff, not about you.

If the build is broken and you know how to fix it, fix it, even when it isn't your code.

Nobody works weekends here. If it starts to feel like you have to, that's a planning failure somewhere upstream of you, and we want to hear about it early.

Your manager will come find you around 3pm for half an hour. Bring whatever confused you today. Confusion on day one is information about our documentation, and we collect it.

VPN, passwords, and the on-call rotation are all in the handbook. The rest you'll pick up by asking.

Glad you're here.
