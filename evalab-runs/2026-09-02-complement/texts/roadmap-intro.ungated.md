## What we're building next

This page is the plan, not a filtered version of it.

We publish the roadmap because the alternative — you guessing, us surprising you — has cost both sides too much. Three of the last four things we shipped got reordered after someone opened an issue and argued us out of our own priorities. That's working as intended.

Here's how to read it. *Now* means engineers are on it and it lands this quarter. *Next* means we've committed to the problem but not yet to the shape of the answer. *Exploring* means we're not certain it's ours to solve, and we'd rather hear from you than spend six months finding out. Dates are estimates. We miss them. When we do, the card moves and picks up a note explaining why.

Two things you won't find here: security work in flight, and anything a customer asked us to keep off a public page. Everything else is on the board, including the items we've been quietly avoiding for a year.

Comment on any card. We read all of them and reply to most, and when one changes our minds, we say so on the card.

Swap the "three of the last four" line for a real example — the specificity is what makes the paragraph land, and a made-up number will get caught.
