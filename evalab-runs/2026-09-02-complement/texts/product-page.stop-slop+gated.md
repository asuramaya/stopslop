**Ratchet**

Your team already has a chat tool. The question is whether it costs you a day a week.

Ratchet is built for teams that ship code. Threads stay attached to the pull request that started them, so when a reviewer asks why a migration runs before the backfill, the answer lives next to the diff instead of scrolling out of a channel by Thursday. Channels sync with your repos, too. Open a channel from a GitHub or GitLab org and Ratchet mirrors branch events, CI failures, and deploy status into it. Your on-call engineer sees the failing job and the last three commits in one place. Search covers messages, files, and code snippets going back to your first day, with no retention tier and no archive purge at 90 days.

Ratchet runs on-prem or in our cloud. The on-prem build ships as a single container with Postgres and S3-compatible storage, and one of our engineers walks your infra team through the first install.

Import from Slack, Discord, or Mattermost keeps message history, threads, and file attachments. Most teams under 200 people finish the migration in an afternoon, and we give you a sandbox workspace first so you can run the import twice and check what came across.
