# Roadmap

This page lists what we plan to build over the next three quarters, roughly in the order we expect to ship it. We publish it because most of you are building on top of our SDK, and planning your own work against a black box is miserable.

The board has three columns. Items in Now are staffed and in progress, with a target month attached. Next means designed and queued, but the month can move. Later means we agree the problem is worth solving and have not committed to a shape yet. Anything in Later can be cut, and we will say so on this page when it is. Dates slip too. When they do, we update the item and note the reason instead of quietly sliding it forward, so you can watch the changelog at the bottom for every edit since January.

Security patches, infrastructure work, and anything covered by a customer NDA all stay off the page. All of it ships without notice, so do not read an empty roadmap as a quiet quarter.

If something you depend on is missing, open a discussion in the roadmap repo and link it to the closest existing item. We read every one before quarterly planning, and about a third of what reaches the Now column started there.
