Senior Backend Engineer, Payments
Chicago or remote (US) · $185k–$225k + equity

We move about 4 million payouts a month for small businesses that can't wait three days for money to land. Forty engineers, half on the platform side. You'd join the team that owns the ledger and the settlement pipeline.

The stack is a ledger service in Go backed by Postgres, an event log on Kafka, and a set of bank integrations that all behave slightly differently and none of which are documented well. Most of the interesting problems are about correctness under partial failure. A wire goes out, the confirmation never comes back, and the reconciliation job has to work out the truth from three sources that disagree.

We want someone who has run systems where being wrong costs real money. That usually means six or more years writing backend services, comfort with SQL past the ORM, and enough operational scar tissue to argue for boring technology. Go experience helps, but we've hired from Java, Rust, and Python. Payments background is not required; patience for regulatory detail is.

You'd also carry the pager one week in six, review other people's designs, and sometimes tell product no. That last part matters more than it sounds.

Interviewing takes three weeks in most cases. It starts with a 45-minute call with the hiring manager, then a paid take-home you can finish in two hours, then a half-day loop, on-site or video. We give real feedback either way, including to candidates we turn down.

Apply with a resume and a paragraph about a system you fixed.
