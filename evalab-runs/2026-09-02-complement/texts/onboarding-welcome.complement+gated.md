# Welcome

You made it. Coffee is in the kitchen on the second floor, and the good machine is the one on the left.

Your first week has no deliverables. We mean that. The work you ship in month three depends on how well you understand the system in week one, so spend the time reading code, asking questions, and breaking things in staging.

Here is what to do today. Your laptop should already have the repo cloned and the dev environment running. If `make dev` fails, that is normal and it is our fault, not yours. File a ticket in #eng-onboarding with the error and someone will fix the setup script within the day. That ticket is genuinely useful to us.

Then find your onboarding buddy on the team page. They have blocked out two hours this afternoon for you. Ask them the questions that feel too basic to ask, because those are usually the ones our documentation answers badly. After that, read the architecture overview in the handbook, and then read one recent pull request from your team end to end. The overview tells you what we intended. The pull request tells you what we actually built.

A few things about how we work. Code review here is slow and thorough, and nobody takes it personally. We deploy on Fridays because our rollback is good. If you find a comment that lies about the code beneath it, fix the comment, and no ticket needed.

By the end of week two, we would like you to have merged something small and deleted something dead. Glad you are here.
