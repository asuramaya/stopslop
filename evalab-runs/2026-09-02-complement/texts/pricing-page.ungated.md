## Pricing

Every plan reviews unlimited repositories. You pay for people, not lines of code.

### Solo — $0

For one developer. Public repos, plus one private one.

Full review on every pull request. Inline comments, not a wall of text at the end. You get the same analysis engine everyone else gets — the paid tiers add scale and control, never smarter reviews.

No card. No trial clock counting down in the corner.

### Team — $19 per developer / month

For teams up to 50.

Private repos, all of them. Reviews land in about 90 seconds on a 500-line diff; we publish the p95 on our status page, and it's been under three minutes since March.

You write the rules. A `review.toml` in the repo root turns checks on and off per directory, so the generated migrations folder stops getting lectured about naming. Slack and Linear hooks are included. So is the audit log.

Support is email, and a human answers within one business day.

### Enterprise — talk to us

Self-hosted, air-gapped if you need it. SSO through SAML or OIDC, SCIM provisioning, role-based access down to the repository.

Custom rule packs written against your internal style guide. A named engineer who knows your codebase. Contractual uptime, invoicing on your terms, and a security questionnaire we've already filled out — ask and we'll send it before the first call.

Pricing starts at 100 seats.
