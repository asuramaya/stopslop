# About Cadence Health

We started Cadence in 2019 after watching a front-desk coordinator at a family practice in Bend rebuild the next day's schedule by hand. Twice. A provider had called out sick, and her software could only move one patient at a time, through a window that took four clicks to close.

That's the problem we work on. Not "appointments" in the abstract — the Tuesday morning when two clinicians are out, the waitlist runs 40 names deep, and somebody has to decide who gets called first.

Today about 900 clinics run their schedules on Cadence, mostly primary care, orthopedics, and behavioral health, in practices from two providers up to sixty. We do templating, waitlist automation, self-scheduling, recall, and the reminder texts that cut no-shows by roughly a fifth in the first quarter after rollout. We integrate with Epic, athenahealth, eClinicalWorks, and Elation. We are a business associate under HIPAA and we sign the agreement before you send us a single record.

What we don't do: charge per provider seat, lock your data behind an export fee, or sell you an implementation package to make the product work as advertised.

Most of our twenty-eight people came out of clinic operations rather than software. Our head of product spent nine years as a practice manager in Sacramento. When you call support, the person picking up has probably worked a front desk.

We're in Portland, Oregon. Come see the product on a Thursday demo, or send us the schedule you're fighting with and we'll tell you honestly whether we'd help.
